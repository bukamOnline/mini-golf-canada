Privacy Policy

How Mini Golf Canada handles analytics, browser location, advertising technology and contact information.

Effective date: July 28, 2026

This policy describes information handled by Mini Golf Canada, a website owned by Nocturnal Devs. Questions or privacy requests can be sent to nocturnaldevs@gmail.com.

Information collected automatically

The hosting provider may record standard technical logs such as IP address, requested page, date and time, browser information and error data for security and reliable delivery. Mini Golf Canada uses Google Analytics with region-aware Google Consent Mode to understand aggregate activity such as page views, traffic sources, approximate region, device category and interactions with directory features. Standard analytics measurement operates outside configured consent regions, unless the visitor declines it. Analytics storage is denied by default in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, Switzerland, United States and Quebec; Google may receive cookieless measurement signals instead of setting analytics cookies before a choice in those regions.

To determine whether the regional consent prompt should be shown, the browser requests coarse country and province or region information from GeoJS. Mini Golf Canada reads only the country and region fields, stores only a temporary consent-region-or-standard result for the browser session, and does not send the response to its own server. Like any internet service, GeoJS receives the requesting IP address; its published policy says it does not keep access logs or use cookies.

Cookies and similar technologies

Analytics and advertising services may use cookies, local storage, pixels or similar identifiers. The regional prompt appears automatically in the site's configured consent regions, while the footer privacy control remains available everywhere. An explicit choice is stored in the visitor's browser. Analytics defaults are region-aware, and advertising storage remains denied unless an appropriate consent choice is provided. See the cookie policy for categories and controls.

Advertising

Mini Golf Canada may display advertising supplied by third-party vendors, including Google. When advertising is active, Google and its partners may use cookies or device identifiers to serve and measure ads. Advertising may be personalized based on prior visits where the law and the visitor's consent allow it, or non-personalized/contextual where required.

Visitors can manage Google advertising personalization through Google Ads Settings. Additional industry opt-out information is available through YourAdChoices. Where required, a Google-certified consent management platform will present additional advertising choices before advertising storage is used.

Location search

The Use my location feature runs only after the visitor clicks the button and grants browser permission. The latitude, longitude and accuracy supplied by the browser are used in the page to calculate distance and order relevant listings. Mini Golf Canada does not send that precise position to its own server or store it in the directory. A browser or network provider may handle location according to its own settings and policy.

Information a visitor sends

If someone emails a course submission, correction, advertising request or privacy question, Mini Golf Canada receives the sender's email address and the information included in the message. That information is used to answer the request, verify a change and maintain an appropriate record of the correspondence. Do not send sensitive personal information.

Sharing and retention

Information may be processed by service providers used for hosting, analytics, email, security and advertising. It may also be disclosed when required by law or needed to protect the site and its users. Contact correspondence is retained only as long as reasonably needed for the request, business records, fraud prevention or legal obligations. Aggregate analytics may be retained according to the configured service period.

Children and external links

The directory is for a general audience and is not designed to collect personal information from children. Venue and map links lead to independent websites with their own privacy practices.

Your choices

Use the Privacy choices control in the footer to allow or decline analytics cookies at any time. Browser settings can delete stored preferences, block cookies and revoke location permission. Depending on applicable law, a person may request access, correction or deletion of personal information by emailing nocturnaldevs@gmail.com; identity may need to be verified before a request is completed.

Analytics preferences

Mini Golf Canada uses region-aware analytics to understand which pages help visitors. You can allow or decline analytics cookies; directory features work either way. Privacy details
