Mini Golf Party Budget Calculator

Estimate a mini golf party total using the venue quote, guest count, food, room fees, tax and contingency.

Use the venue's labelled quote

Enter a current mini-golf-only rate or written party quote. Do not substitute a headline bundle unless every guest will use the included activities. Confirm minimum guest counts, non-player charges, gratuity, booking fees, deposits and cancellation terms.

The contingency is a planning cushion, not a venue fee. The result is not a checkout price and is not stored. The party guide lists questions that often change the final total.
