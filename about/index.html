About Mini Golf Canada

Mini Golf Canada helps people compare mini golf and mini putt courses across Canada.

Mini Golf Canada is an independent directory for people trying to choose a mini golf or mini putt course in Canada. It brings addresses, course formats, hours, photos, public rating context and venue-controlled links into one place so families, travellers and groups can make a practical shortlist.

Who owns the site

The site is owned and maintained by Nocturnal Devs, a web and mobile product studio based in Toronto, Ontario. Editorial and technical questions can be sent to nocturnaldevs@gmail.com or (437) 366-2920. Mini Golf Canada is not owned by, operated by or affiliated with the listed courses unless a page explicitly says otherwise.

Why the directory exists

Mini golf information is often spread across venue websites, map profiles, tourism directories and seasonal posts. The directory's purpose is to organize the stable planning facts, show where each profile came from and give visitors a direct path to the venue for current decisions.

What the listings are

Listings are researched summaries, not first-hand reviews. A course page describes the public record available for that specific venue and identifies the date of the source data. It may include a dated public rating snapshot for context, but Mini Golf Canada does not copy individual reviews or present third-party ratings as its own.

Software-assisted workflows help normalize addresses, identify structured venue features and draft source-based summaries. Editorial rules remove irrelevant source navigation, unrelated package prices and unsupported claims. Pages are checked for source links, duplicate copy, broken structure and technical integrity before publication. The full process is described in the editorial policy.

How to use the site

Browse by province, city or the nearby search. Use Mini Golf Canada to compare likely options, then confirm same-day hours, admission, booking and accessibility with the selected venue. Every listing includes a correction route because business information can change after publication.
