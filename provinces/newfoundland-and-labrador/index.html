How to narrow the Newfoundland and Labrador list

Start with the city or town closest to the outing, then compare the course setting and the date attached to each profile. Of the 2 current entries, 1 include a weekly schedule and 1 have venue, municipal, tourism or other explicit source support. A missing schedule is a reason to verify directly, not evidence that a course is closed.

Use photos to understand the setting, but use the venue for operational decisions. Outdoor courses can close for weather, indoor entertainment centres can reserve space for groups, and multi-activity properties may sell mini golf separately from an attraction pass.

Questions to resolve before travelling

  1. Which Newfoundland and Labrador course is open at the exact time you plan to arrive?
  2. What is the current mini-golf-only admission for each player age, including taxes and booking fees?
  3. Does the full course route meet the group's mobility, seating, lighting and washroom needs?
  4. Are food, arcade credits or other activities separate from the mini golf ticket?
