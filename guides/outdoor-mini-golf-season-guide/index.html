Outdoor Mini Golf Season and Weather Guide

How to plan seasonal outdoor mini golf around rain, heat, daylight and changing venue schedules.

By Mini Golf Canada Research DeskUpdated September 7, 2026

A seasonal opening range is not a promise that every date in it is playable. Canadian course sources use holiday windows, school schedules, weather conditions and daily status tables differently. These examples show what to verify before an autumn or shoulder-season trip.

Operator publications and local-authority sources checked September 7, 2026. Planning analysis by the Mini Golf Canada Research Desk; not firsthand visits or guaranteed current offers.

Woodstock: a holiday window with a weather condition

Coast to Coast publishes Victoria Day weekend through Thanksgiving weekend, weather permitting. That is useful for deciding whether a trip is in the expected season, but it is not a year-round schedule or assurance of play during rain. Ask about the particular day and final start time before making a special autumn journey. The operator's midweek discount is not evidence the course opens through weather interruptions.

Sources: Coast to Coast season.

Victoria: school holidays are a clue, not daily hours

Mattick's Farm welcomes the 2026 season and gives school holidays as a general opening guideline, with weather-related updates. A weekday once school has resumed therefore needs its own hours check. Use the miniature-course contact rather than a neighbouring full-size golf booking. The outdoor forest setting does not establish that paths remain open or playable under all weather conditions.

Sources: Mattick's Farm seasonal guidance.

Calgary: an activity-status table overrides assumptions

WinSport's retrieved table labels mini-golf Closed. That observation is not proof of permanent closure, but it is enough to prevent treating the course as currently available. Other activities at the same venue can be operating at the same time. Check the latest named activity status and ask about a reopening date, rather than using a general summer-activities page title as confirmation.

Sources: WinSport activity status.

Lloydminster: all seasons describes the park

Discover Lloydminster places The Putting Zone among summer activities at Bud Miller All Seasons Park. Its indoor aquatic centre, trails and other year-round amenities do not establish winter golf. This is a useful example of checking the activity itself instead of inferring a mini-golf season from a parent property's name. Confirm the golf kiosk before combining it with another park activity.

Sources: Bud Miller park activity guide.

Before leaving, resolve operation and interruption separately

Ask whether the course will admit players on your date, when the last round starts and what happens if conditions interrupt a paid round. The sources above do not establish one common refund or rain-check policy. Keep the answer from the selected operator with your booking. An indoor alternative should have its own confirmed capacity and age rules, not just an indoor tag.
