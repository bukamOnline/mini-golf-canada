Indoor and Glow Mini Golf in Canada

What to look for when comparing indoor glow golf, black-light mini putt, and entertainment-centre courses.

By Mini Golf Canada Editorial TeamUpdated July 28, 2026

Quick answer: indoor and glow mini golf overlap, but they are not identical. Confirm that the whole course is indoors, then check lighting, sound, booking, accessibility and other activities against the needs of your group.

Indoor does not always mean glow

An indoor course may use ordinary lighting and a traditional theme. A glow course may use black lights, fluorescent paint, music and darker rooms. Some venues combine indoor and outdoor holes, while others place a compact course inside a larger entertainment centre. The most useful listing language describes the setting and experience separately instead of treating every indoor course as glow golf.

Questions to answer before booking

Indoor and glow golf comparison points
TopicQuestion to ask
EnvironmentIs every hole climate controlled, or does part of the route go outside?
Lighting and soundDoes the venue use darkness, flashing effects, loud music or animated props?
Round formatHow many holes are included, and is play timed during busy periods?
AccessAre the course route, entrance and washrooms usable for the people in your group?
BookingAre walk-ins accepted, or are time slots required on weekends?
ExtrasAre arcade credits, laser tag, bowling or food bundled with admission?

Plan for different ages and sensory needs

Dark rooms and dramatic effects can be exciting for one player and uncomfortable for another. Families should ask about visibility, music volume, moving props and whether a player can leave the course partway through. For mobility planning, a general accessible-entrance label does not necessarily describe every hole. Contact the venue for the route, slopes, narrow passages, seating and washroom details that matter to the group.

Allow time beyond the round

A round can take longer when the course is busy, a group has young players, or the venue spaces groups at each hole. Entertainment centres can add check-in, waivers, food and arcade time. When an event has a fixed end time, ask the venue for its current estimate rather than relying on a generic mini golf duration.

Read prices as packages

Indoor venues often sell activity bundles. Make sure a displayed amount is for mini golf itself and not a multi-attraction pass. Check taxes, age bands, required socks or equipment, replay pricing and expiry rules.
