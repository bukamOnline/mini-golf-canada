Indoor and Glow Mini Golf in Canada

What to look for when comparing indoor glow golf, black-light mini putt, and entertainment-centre courses.

By Mini Golf Canada Research DeskUpdated October 28, 2026

Plan a winter or rainy-day mini-golf outing around the actual course, ticket and entry rules. Compare four named indoor options below, then check the details that affect your party: non-player admission, repeat rounds, lighting, footwear and the current booking window.

The four-course comparison and Halifax timetable were checked October 28, 2026. Other examples retain their September 7, 2026 source check unless a section states otherwise. Planning analysis by the Mini Golf Canada Research Desk; not firsthand visits or guaranteed current offers.

These examples cover Kelowna, Calgary, Niagara Falls and Halifax. They are starting points for different regions, not a ranking or a promise of same-day capacity. Each profile links to its operator.

Compare the format before counting eighteen

Le Grand in Quebec City has fifteen putting holes followed by three mini-hockey holes, whereas Wizards' Golf in Niagara Falls describes eighteen glow-golf holes. Both can fill an indoor outing, but Le Grand is not eighteen conventional putting holes. Its operator's 90-120-minute estimate also makes it a different planning commitment from squeezing golf between other appointments. Tell the group which activity they are booking instead of using a generic eighteen-hole label.

Sources: Le Grand format and time; Wizards' Golf course.

A two-round ticket is not a multi-attraction day pass

Speeders Chinook in Calgary lists one blacklight golf round at $12 or two at $20 per person. For two people who want two rounds, that is $40 instead of $48 in single-round tickets. Its larger day pass combines races, laser tag, one golf round, timed activities and arcade credit. The larger pass does not buy more putting than the two-round golf ticket; choose it only when the other activities suit your plan and their eligibility rules are satisfied. Confirm taxes and fees at checkout.

Sources: Speeders Chinook ticket inclusions.

Glow decor does not resolve sensory suitability

Haunted Manor describes two air-powered effects and allows staff to identify them before you play. Fluo S'amuse distinguishes its wheelchair-usable first route from its second route. Those specific disclosures are more useful than assuming every indoor venue is equally comfortable or playable. Ask about the light, sound, unexpected effects and route that matter to your group; no sensory audit or physical access inspection was conducted for this guide.

Sources: Haunted Manor effects FAQ; Fluo route access.

An accompanying person may still need admission

Putting Edge's Halifax and Scarborough pages require paid entry for everyone who goes onto the course. A non-playing adult is therefore part of the head count. Both branches are cashless and recommend reservations where walk-in space is limited. That is a branch-source check, not a policy assumed from an indoor label; other operators may sell separate spectator tickets.

Sources: Halifax entry policy; Scarborough entry policy.

Check the winter visit window before paying

Halifax Putting Edge now publishes a regular timetable: noon–10 p.m. Monday–Thursday, noon–midnight Friday, 10 a.m.–midnight Saturday and 10 a.m.–10 p.m. Sunday. School holidays may have early openings. The operator allows the last game to start at closing; use that branch-specific rule rather than assuming every indoor course does the same. Reserve for your date, since indoor shelter does not guarantee space.

Sources: Halifax regular hours and admission.

An indoor summer pop-up may become simulator golf in winter

OVG in Kelowna is an example: the operator’s current site and embedded updates describe the return from the summer putt lounge to winter simulator golf. An indoor address alone does not establish a year-round physical mini-golf course. Scandia separately advertises year-round indoor putting in the same city. Choose the activity first, then its available booking.
