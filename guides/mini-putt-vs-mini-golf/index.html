Mini Putt vs Mini Golf in Canada

Mini putt, mini golf, putt putt, and glow golf often describe the same family-friendly putting activity.

By Mini Golf Canada Editorial TeamUpdated July 31, 2026

Quick answer: mini golf and mini putt usually point to the same basic activity in Canadian searches. Venue names vary, so use both terms when searching and then compare the actual course format.

Why the names vary

Operators choose wording based on local habit, branding and the style of the venue. A business may use Mini-Putt in its name while describing the activity as miniature golf on its website. Another may advertise glow golf, adventure golf or putting even though visitors are looking for the same short-course game. The wording alone does not tell you whether the course is indoors, outdoors, themed or suitable for young children.

Search by intent after the basic term

Adding a city or practical need produces a better shortlist than repeating synonyms. Useful combinations include indoor mini golf in a city, outdoor mini putt near a destination, glow golf for a date night, or mini golf birthday packages. The directory's search also recognizes venue features such as arcade games, food and accessibility information where those details were available from public sources.

Do not infer the experience from the name

A whimsical name can belong to a simple outdoor course, and a plain business name can hide a highly themed indoor layout. Check source images and the venue description. Look for whether mini golf is the main attraction or an add-on at a bowling centre, campground, driving range, resort or amusement property. This distinction often affects parking, ticket bundles, crowd patterns and how much time a group may spend on site.

How Mini Golf Canada labels listings

The directory keeps official business names in page titles and headings. It uses mini golf and mini putt in navigation where either phrase helps people find the activity, but it does not rename a venue. Course profiles separate the venue category, mini golf format and extra activities so a visitor can understand why a business appears in the directory.

Start with the province directory or city directory, then open the individual venue sources before travelling. If a business has changed its name or no longer offers mini golf, use the correction link on its page.
