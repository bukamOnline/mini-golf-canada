Mini Golf Tournament Formats and Planning

Set up teams, timing, scorekeeping and fair tie-breakers for a casual mini golf tournament.

By Mini Golf Canada Research DeskUpdated August 7, 2026

Quick answer: use stroke play for a simple individual event, team best-ball for mixed abilities, or match play for a small bracket. Publish the stroke limit and tie-breaker before play begins.

Pick a format that fits the group

Common mini golf event formats
FormatHow it worksBest fit
Stroke playLowest total across all holes wins.Easy scoring and one full-field result
Team best-ballRecord the lowest team score on each hole.Mixed abilities and social events
ScrambleEveryone putts, then the team continues from its chosen ball.Cooperation, if the venue permits ball placement
Match playWin individual holes rather than adding every stroke.Small brackets and head-to-head play
Skills awardsRecognize aces, consistency or a chosen challenge hole.Low-pressure family and workplace groups

Set four rules in writing

Define the maximum strokes per hole, out-of-bounds procedure, ball-relief distance from walls and whether practice strokes are allowed. Use the venue's posted rules when they differ. A common casual maximum is six strokes, but the organizer should not assume the course uses it.

Manage flow

Teams of three or four usually balance pace and social play. Start teams at timed intervals or, with venue permission, on different holes. Give every team the same order and explain where completed scorecards go. Ask the operator whether the event can reserve the course or must move among public groups.

Choose a tie-breaker before scores exist

Options include the lowest back-nine total, fewest sixes, most holes-in-one, or a designated playoff hole. Avoid inventing a rule after seeing who tied. For a family event, co-winners may be more useful than delaying everyone for a playoff.

Build the schedule

Add check-in, instructions, the estimated round range, score verification and awards. The tournament scorecard tool creates a printable table in the browser, while the round-time estimator helps set a realistic event window. Confirm group capacity, deposits, food and cancellation terms with the venue in writing.
