Mini Golf Prices in Canada

A planning guide for mini golf admission, family passes, group rates, and add-on entertainment costs.

By Mini Golf Canada Research DeskUpdated September 6, 2026

A family ticket is not always the cheapest ticket. We checked ten Canadian operator rate pages and calculated example outings using their actual age bands, equipment charges and ticket types. The comparisons below explain what the headline amounts leave out.

Research date: September 6, 2026. All amounts are Canadian dollars. These are selected worked examples, not a national average, a cheapest-course ranking or a quote. We did not book or visit the venues. Sources are linked beside each calculation and the venue profiles show the individual rates.

Ten real ticket calculations

Read the party description before comparing totals: a nine-hole ticket, an 18-hole round and natural-grass putting are not identical products. Tax treatment is left exactly as disclosed; an amount labelled before tax should not be compared as a final checkout total.

When the family pass loses

At Riverside Ridge, two adults and two children aged 12 or under cost $44 before tax on individual golf tickets. The family ticket is $45 for up to five. For that four-person party, individual tickets save $1; with a fifth paying player, the family option can become the better buy. The word family does not itself mean a discount for every family size.

At Tap-Ins, two regular tickets plus two junior tickets total $64 before tax, against $58 for the matching family package. That saves $6. But putters are a separate rental: four add $8 before tax. A $58 headline therefore becomes $66 before tax for that family when everyone rents. Bringing suitable equipment, where permitted, changes the comparison.

Do not turn an unclear package into a promise

Centennial and Peribonka advertise family prices, but the rate text we retrieved does not define the exact party composition. We calculate individual admission in the table instead of assuming that any four people qualify. Ask the operator whether the package covers your player ages and number of adults. The answer can matter more than a small difference in individual rates.

Choose the round before choosing add-ons

Toronto's Par-Tee Putt sells both nine-hole and 18-hole tickets. Moving from $14.95 to $24.95 adds $10 per player, or $40 for four. That is a choice about how much golf you want, not evidence that one venue is overpriced. At Le Rigolfeur, the jump from golf alone to the karting combination is larger still. Exclude an activity your group cannot or will not use before judging a bundle's value.

Age bands are not standardized

A child-rate comparison can change on a birthday. Within Range uses a junior band for ages 13-17 and begins its senior band at 55; Timmins lists seniors from 65. Use the ages on the visit date. When a source does not explain infants, accompanying non-players or support people, leave the charge unresolved and ask rather than assigning zero.

Keep the final comparison honest

For each venue, add only the tickets your party needs, necessary equipment, disclosed taxes and required fees. Keep food, travel and optional activities separate. Then check the outing's constraints: a lower price is not helpful if the last mini-golf admission is earlier than your arrival, a seasonal course has closed, or the route does not meet your group's access needs.

The party budget calculator can total a quote for a larger event. For a regular outing, use the individual rate table on the linked course page and confirm the final amount with the operator. We update a rate only when a source supports that particular ticket, not by applying another venue's price.
