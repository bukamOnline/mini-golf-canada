Mini Golf Prices in Canada

A planning guide for mini golf admission, family passes, group rates, and add-on entertainment costs.

By Mini Golf Canada Editorial TeamUpdated July 31, 2026

Quick answer: there is no single Canada-wide mini golf price. Compare the exact ticket type, player ages, taxes, date and included activities. Treat directory amounts as dated references and use the venue's checkout or phone line for the final total.

Why a headline price can be misleading

Mini golf businesses use several pricing models. A stand-alone outdoor course may list one round by age. An entertainment centre may lead with a multi-attraction bundle, while a resort or campground may restrict access to guests. Seasonal promotions and replay discounts add another layer. Two amounts are not comparable until you know what each ticket includes.

Calculate the group total

Write down the number of players in each age band, then multiply by the current venue rate. Add taxes and required fees before comparing another course. If a bundle is involved, compare it with the activities the group will realistically use, not the advertised number of inclusions. For a birthday, include non-player rules, food, room time and the deposit.

A hypothetical family total should be labelled as an estimate because a venue can change age cutoffs or prices at any time. The final checkout screen or a written group quote is the reliable figure.

Why some listings say to confirm prices

Mini Golf Canada intentionally leaves out an amount when the available text is old, unlabelled, mixed with a hotel or attraction bundle, or unclear about whether it applies to mini golf. Publishing a confident but wrong number would be less useful than directing a visitor to the current source. Where labelled prices are shown, the page states that they are dated reference amounts.

Watch for seasonal differences

Outdoor courses can change rates and hours between the shoulder season and peak summer. Indoor venues may use weekday specials or timed bookings. Coupon pages and old social posts are particularly easy to mistake for current standard admission. Check the date and make sure the offer appears on a venue-controlled channel.

Use price as one part of the decision

A lower ticket can still be the wrong fit if the course is far away, closed for weather or unsuitable for the group. Compare location, setting, number of holes, accessibility, booking and recent official details alongside admission. Start from a province page, a city page or the nearby search, then confirm the selected venue's current total.
