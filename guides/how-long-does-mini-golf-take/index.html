How Long Does Mini Golf Take?

Planning ranges for mini golf round length by holes, group size, pace and queue conditions.

By Mini Golf Canada Research DeskUpdated August 7, 2026

Quick answer: an uncrowded round often needs roughly 25 to 45 minutes for nine holes and 45 to 75 minutes for 18 holes. Group size, queues, children, scoring and course design can move the result well outside those planning ranges.

Use a range, not a promise

Round time is the sum of play at each hole, walking between holes and waiting for the group ahead. A compact indoor layout can move quickly until a bottleneck forms; a landscaped outdoor route can add walking even when play is smooth. Venue estimates are more useful than a universal average when an event has a fixed end time.

Group9 holes18 holes

Five or six40-60 minutes65-95 minutes

These are planning assumptions created by Mini Golf Canada, not measured promises from every venue. Add at least 15 minutes for arrival, payment, club selection and instructions. Add more for waivers, food, arcade credits or a party room.

What slows a round

  • Large groups taking every stroke in sequence instead of preparing while another player putts.
  • Young players replaying shots or needing help retrieving balls and reading obstacles.
  • Score discussion, photos and competitive tie-breakers.
  • A difficult hole that repeatedly reaches the venue's stroke limit.
  • Closely spaced tee times or a group ahead that cannot be passed.

Plan an event backwards

Begin with the time everyone must leave. Reserve the final 10 to 15 minutes for regrouping, then subtract food or awards, the round range, and arrival time. Ask the venue whether a large party will play as one group or several teams; multiple teams start faster but need a clear meeting point afterward.

The round-time estimator applies these assumptions to a chosen hole count, player count and pace. It deliberately returns a range and shows the added arrival buffer.
