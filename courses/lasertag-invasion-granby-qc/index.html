Visiting Lasertag Invasion

Miniputt Invasion is an indoor 18-hole blacklight course at Lasertag Invasion in Granby. Golf can be bought alone or combined with laser tag or virtual reality, with different promotion rules for each combination.

Visit details checked September 6, 2026
Booking confirmationReservations are recommended. The operator asks customers to wait for a response before treating an online request as confirmed. Source
Young childrenAges four and under enter free with a regular-priced admission. Source
VR add-onMini-putt is advertised at $7 instead of $11 with a VR rental; promotions cannot be combined. Source

Our planning take

Choose the $11 standalone round for a golf-only visit. If you already intend to rent VR, the golf add-on saves $4 per player, but do not stack another discount onto it or assume a submitted booking request guarantees a start.
