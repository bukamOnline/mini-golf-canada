Visiting Glowtown

Glowtown's Neon North course uses Canadian landmarks and glowing scenery for an indoor putting outing in Stouffville. Mini-golf, arcade play and multiplayer virtual reality share the venue but have separate booking choices.

Visit details checked September 7, 2026

Local entranceThe venue is at 5892 Main Street, Unit 102. Custom group enquiries use fun@glowtown.ca or 905-591-9577. Source

Our planning take

For children under seven, select golf rather than assuming they can join every activity in a multi-activity visit. Reserve Neon North when timing matters, and check whether arcade credits or VR are included in the particular checkout product. The homepage identifies its opening table as summer hours, so recheck it for a later-season visit.
