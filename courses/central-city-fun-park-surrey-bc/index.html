Visiting Central City Fun Park

Central City Fun Park is free to enter, but activities use paid Fun Card credits. Mini-golf belongs to this credit-based attraction system, so free building entry does not mean a free round or an unlimited-activity admission.

Visit details checked September 7, 2026
Card purchaseCards and credits are bought in person. The FAQ says unaffiliated online gift products such as Giftly cannot be redeemed. Source
Walk-in systemIndividual attractions operate first come, first served; online reservations are for parties, not a reserved miniature-golf start. Source
Arrival and foodGuests can use free stalls marked FUN PARK. Outside food is restricted, with limited exceptions for booked party rooms. Source

Our planning take

Ask for the current mini-golf credit charge before loading a card. A $10 purchase gives 25 credits plus GST, while $30 gives 80 credits, but that bonus is applied per card. Decide whether one shared card is permitted for your group before splitting a purchase and potentially losing the bonus.

Tickets and budget

The public text gives card conversion rates but not a readable mini-golf credit cost; a dollar price per round is not established.
