Visiting Mini-Putt Baie-Comeau

Baie-Comeau Tourism describes this as a traditional RDS-style mini-putt at 860 Boulevard Rene-Belanger. The same page also advertises the city's conventional golf club, but that is a separate destination with its own website.

Visit details checked September 7, 2026
Style of playThe local tourism description emphasizes the classic miniature-putting style associated with Quebec's televised RDS courses. Source
Meal pairingCreperie de la Reine is named as a lunch option after a round. Source
Course contactThe mini-putt section gives 418-589-1777 and specifically instructs visitors to call for opening hours. Source

Our planning take

Call the mini-putt number rather than booking at Club de golf de Baie-Comeau through the neighbouring website link. Confirm the creperie's meal service separately if lunch is central to the plan. The visitor bureau's June-to-September office season at the bottom of the page is not the mini-putt operating calendar.

Tickets and budget

Tourism confirms the mini-putt identity and contact, but no admission rate.
