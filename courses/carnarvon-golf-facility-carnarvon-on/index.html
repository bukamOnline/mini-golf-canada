Visiting Carnarvon Golf Facility

Carnarvon Golf Facility's operator confirms new ownership and mini-putt operation for the 2026 season. The miniature activity is separate from its nine-hole par-three course and driving range.

Visit details checked September 7, 2026
Finding the entranceThe operator places the facility at Highways 118 and 35, with the range behind Jug City and the LCBO. Source
Current ownershipThe website explicitly describes reopening under new ownership for 2026. Source
Separate activitiesMini-putt, the range and a nine-hole par-three course are listed as different activities. Source

Our planning take

Use the highway intersection and shop landmarks to find the facility, then ask for mini-putt check-in. Confirm the current owner's prices rather than using an older rate for the previous business, and do not assume the miniature course has nine holes because the regulation course does.

Tickets and budget

The new owner's homepage does not provide a mini-putt-only admission amount.
