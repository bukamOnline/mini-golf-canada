Visiting Magic Mountain GolfZone

Magic Mountain's GolfZone is divided into four differently themed nine-hole courses, rather than one continuous 36-hole round. Peak Station uses railway props, Miner's Canyon has mining obstacles, Tombstone Flats has a cemetery theme, and Critter Ridge features animal scenery.

Visit details checked September 7, 2026
Choosing a routeThe four options are Peak Station, Miner's Canyon, Tombstone Flats and Critter Ridge. Source
Weather policyThe park's rain-check policy requires an entire-park suspension lasting more than 60 minutes; an individual attraction closure does not automatically qualify. Source
Claim before leavingQualifying rain checks must be requested before leaving and within 30 minutes after operations resume. They are valid for the remainder of the season; refunds are not offered under this policy. Source

Our planning take

Choose the number of nine-hole routes your group wants before buying tickets, and confirm which routes the ticket includes. On a disrupted weather day, speak to staff before leaving the property, since returning later to request a rain check may miss the stated claim window.
