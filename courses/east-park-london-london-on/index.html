Visiting East Park London

East Park offers Harry's Goofy Golf and the Golf Zone as separate choices or a two-course ticket. Harry's is the traditional course; the Golf Zone uses a sand-filled synthetic surface designed to play more like a regulation putting green.

Our planning take

For adults and the older child/senior band, playing both courses adds $2.50 per player. A family with two adults and two children aged 5-12 therefore pays $10 more before tax for both. That can be good value if you have time for a second course, but is wasted if your schedule permits only one.
