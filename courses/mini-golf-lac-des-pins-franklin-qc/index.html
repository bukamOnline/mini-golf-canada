Visiting Mini-Golf Lac Des Pins

Camping Lac des Pins publishes two different mini-putt choices, La Jungle and Le Defi, with separate adult and child prices. Those golf fees appear alongside campground visitor admission, so non-campers should confirm whether both charges apply.

Our planning take

Two adults and two qualifying children total $17.80 for La Jungle or $27.80 for Le Defi, a $10 difference in the golf portion. Ask whether an outside visitor also pays the campground entry fee before comparing this total with a standalone course.
