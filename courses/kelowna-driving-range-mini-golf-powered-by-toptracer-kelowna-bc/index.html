Visiting Kelowna Driving Range & Mini Golf Powered by Toptracer

Kelowna Driving Range's miniature course has 18 holes and includes a complimentary second round with admission. The golf ticket includes a putter and ball; the site's Toptracer tokens and Trackman session rates instead apply to its practice and simulator services.

Our planning take

Two adults and two qualifying youths have a $68 subtotal, covering up to two rounds each under the replay offer. Check the youth and senior cutoffs and whether the second round must be continuous. Year-round simulator operation does not establish winter availability for the outdoor course.
