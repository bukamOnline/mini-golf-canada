Visiting Deb's Greenhouse & Mini Golf

Deb's Greenhouse has a Wonderland-themed mini-golf attraction described by Family Fun Edmonton as Down the Rabbit Hole. Its reported family offer and young-child condition are more specific than general greenhouse admission, but the operator's current booking terms were not readable. Treat the local guide's offer as a planning lead to confirm, not a guaranteed ticket price.

Our planning take

Before counting on the reported family offer, give Deb's the ages and number of players and ask for the full group price. The young-child condition involves an accompanying player aged twelve or older, not simply any paying child. Also distinguish a mini-golf booking from a greenhouse visit or a separately promoted seasonal event; neither automatically includes the other.

Tickets and budget

A local family guide reports a starting $25 family-of-four offer and a conditional under-six concession. These are secondary-source leads, not a verified current operator tariff.
