Visiting Homestead Adventure Park

Homestead's 18-hole outdoor course uses Quinte West's history as its theme. It sits among the park's high-ropes challenges, but golf and ropes have different published operating windows.

Visit details checked September 7, 2026
Local themeThe operator describes the miniature course as a journey through Quinte West history. Source
Different activity hoursThe published schedule ends high ropes at 5 p.m., while mini-putt and remote-control boats continue until 9 p.m. Source
Separate scaleThe site lists 16 high-ropes challenges; that number is not the miniature-golf hole count. Source

Our planning take

For a combined outing, schedule ropes before the earlier 5 p.m. cutoff and golf afterwards, subject to current seasonal hours. Confirm the combination ticket and last admissions for each activity instead of assuming the later golf closing applies to the entire park.

Tickets and budget

The homepage does not establish a golf-only or combined-activity ticket total.
