Visiting VicSquare Minigolf

VicSquare's year-round indoor course puts 18 glow-in-the-dark holes among world-landmark scenery, including the CN Tower, Big Ben and Golden Gate Bridge. The mall's tenant page supplies golf-specific contact details and opening times, which differ from the mall's general shopping hours.

Visit details checked September 6, 2026
Golf hoursThe tenant page lists 11 a.m.-9 p.m. Sunday-Thursday and 11 a.m.-10 p.m. Friday-Saturday. Source
Direct contactThe mini-golf number is 306-586-8380, separate from the mall office's phone number. Source
SettingThe course uses illuminated landmark scenes rather than an outdoor grass layout. Source

Our planning take

For an evening visit, call the golf desk about the entrance to use after other stores close and the last round sold. The tenant hours are not a promise that every mall entrance stays unlocked until golf closes.

Tickets and budget

The mall's official tenant page confirms the course but does not publish an admission table. Ask the golf desk for the current player and group rates.
