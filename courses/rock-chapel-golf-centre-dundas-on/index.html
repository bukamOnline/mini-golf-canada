Visiting Rock Chapel Golf Centre

Rock Chapel's miniature golf is an 18-hole real-grass putting course. It is separate from the club's nine-hole par-three course and driving range, offering a short-game experience on living turf rather than artificial carpet.

Visit details checked September 6, 2026
ReservationThe homepage explicitly requires a pre-booked tee time for mini golf. Source
Course identityThe nine-hole designation belongs to the par-three golf course, not the miniature putting route. Source
SettingThe facility is in rural Dundas near the edge of Waterdown. Source

Our planning take

Book the miniature-golf option specifically instead of turning up under an assumed walk-in policy. The real-grass format is worth choosing for a group interested in reading slopes and controlling putting pace; do not expect the play surface of a conventional carpeted mini-putt.
