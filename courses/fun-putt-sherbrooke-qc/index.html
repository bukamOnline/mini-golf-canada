Visiting Fun Putt

Multigolf Sherbrooke offers two distinct 18-hole options: conventional mini-golf and Fun Putt, which combines putting with skill games. The two courses have different adult and child prices and should not be treated as one combined ticket.

Our planning take

For two adults and two children aged 6-12, Fun Putt totals $42.20 before tax versus $50.46 for conventional mini-golf, a difference of $8.26. Choose by the desired game as well as price: skill-based Fun Putt is not just a discounted repeat of the other course.
