Visiting Within Range Golf Centres Inc

Within Range's Burlington location has an 18-hole mini-putt course at 3050 Guelph Line. The mini-putt page is specific to Burlington, so it should not be read as confirmation of the same activity at the operator's Thornhill location.

Our planning take

A 13-year-old falls into the junior rate, not the children's rate. An adult aged 55 already qualifies for the senior band here. Confirm admission for children under four, which the displayed rate table does not resolve.
