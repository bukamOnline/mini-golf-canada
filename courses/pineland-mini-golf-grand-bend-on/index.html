Visiting Pineland Mini Golf

Pineland's distinctive timber entrance sign and posted rules are visible in Cary Horning's August 2025 photographs at Kite King of the Bend Camp Store. The rules identify an eighteen-hole round and a scoring cap, giving families a more concrete picture of play than a generic Grand Bend map pin. The photographs establish the course at that date, not today's opening hours.

Our planning take

Agree on the posted stroke cap before beginning: it keeps a difficult hole from holding up the whole group. Check the board again on arrival because the photograph is dated 2025 and current rules can differ. The equipment-return wording supports a single eighteen-hole circuit; it does not establish free replays or an all-day pass.

Tickets and budget

The photographed sign explains play but does not supply a readable admission tariff. Confirm the current course fee at the camp-store site.
