Visiting Dinosaur valley Mini golf & Josephine's Vegetables

Dinosaur Valley's sixty-three holes are organized as seven nine-hole courses, not one compulsory sixty-three-hole round. Steel-edged putting layouts use angles and curves among large skeletal sculptures, combining unusual bank-shot geometry with a sculpture-garden setting.

Our planning take

Choose the number of nine-hole loops your group wants before requesting a quote. Do not budget for sixty-three holes automatically or infer seven admissions from the layout. Compare the Standard and Dragon Adventure package inclusions with the operator: the two tourism pages describe exhibits but no precise price difference. A September visit particularly needs an hours check.

Tickets and budget

Tourism confirms course combinations but neither linked package has a numerical price or explicit admission allowance.
