Visiting Aventure Granby

Aventure Granby introduced a themed 18-hole mini-putt in 2026 as part of its expansion beyond paintball. Unlike activities that require an advance booking, its mini-putt is explicitly walk-in, with final departures at 7 p.m.

Visit details checked September 7, 2026
Mini-putt bookingThe operator says no reservation is required: visitors should present themselves on-site. Source

Our planning take

Arrive before 7 p.m. with enough time to check in, rather than treating that cutoff as the time to reach the car park. Confirm any paintball or other activity booking separately, and do not plan around attractions the operator still describes as future developments.

Tickets and budget

The reviewed page confirms the course and walk-in rule but does not establish its current single-round fee.
