Visiting Centennial Park Golf Centre

Centennial's landscaped mini-golf course is a separate 18-hole activity within the larger golf centre. The executive golf courses and driving range have different tickets; their nine-hole rates do not apply to mini putt.

Visit details checked September 6, 2026
Round and equipmentThe operator estimates 45-60 minutes and supplies putters, balls and scorecards. Source
ArrivalMini-putt start times do not need pre-booking; the operator advises checking weekend availability. Source
Last admissionMini-putt tickets stop selling one hour before the pro shop closes, not at closing time. Source

Our planning take

For an evening visit, work backwards from the ticket cutoff rather than the golf centre's closing time. Ask which players qualify for the family ticket before comparing it with individual admission.
