Visiting Joe's Mini Golf

Joe's welcomes non-campers to its 18-hole course at Summerland RV Park, with no tee-time booking required. The stated season runs from mid-May to mid-September; the nominal 7 p.m. last round can change, so it is not a dependable late-arrival guarantee.

Visit details checked September 6, 2026
PetsDogs cannot enter the mini-golf course, but may stay on the nearby patio with a supervisor. Source
Wednesday offerSix players cost $70 in one payment on Wednesdays; additional players are $12 each, with no combining other offers. Source
Age categoriesAdults are ages 13-59, children 5-12, and seniors 60 and over; ages four and under play free. Source

Our planning take

Six adults save $14 with the Wednesday offer, but two adults and four children cost only $72 at regular rates, making their saving $2. A dog also needs someone to remain on the patio, which can change how many people will actually play.
