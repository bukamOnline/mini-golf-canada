Visiting Goodwood Family Golf Centre

Goodwood's 18-hole mini-putt price depends on the number of players, with equipment and tax included. It operates first come, first served; a reserved party space is a separate charge and does not itself buy golf admission.

Visit details checked September 7, 2026
Included equipmentThe golf price includes balls, putters and a scorecard for 18 holes. Source
ReplayThe operator advertises another round at half the initial cost. Source
Young childrenChildren under four play free. Source

Our planning take

Four paid players cost $28 including tax at the four-to-nine-person rate, with a further $14 for a half-price replay. Ask how free under-four players count toward the group threshold. Any reserved event space must be budgeted in addition to the golf.
