Visiting Mini-Golf Disraeli

Mini-Golf Disraeli is an 18-hole course at Parc de la Gare beside Lake Aylmer, with a lakeside terrace and dairy bar. The surrounding site also offers water activities, making it possible to plan golf as one part of a longer outdoor stop.

Visit details checked September 6, 2026
SeasonThe operator describes the golf season as May through October. Source
Group sizeGroups of 20 or more can arrange customized visits, from golf alone to packages with catering or a marquee. Source
Beyond golfKayak and paddleboard rentals are advertised separately; the page also identifies free beach access. Source

Our planning take

Keep watercraft rental costs separate from your golf budget, and agree on the exact inclusions for a catered group event. For a quieter round, check the Friday music schedule before combining the course and terrace.
