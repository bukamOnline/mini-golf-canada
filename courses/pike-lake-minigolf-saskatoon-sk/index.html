Visiting Pike Lake Minigolf

Pike Lake Minigolf occupies the southeast corner of the main parking lot at Pike Lake Provincial Park. The 18-hole course uses an on-call opening arrangement in its current notice, making a phone or text check more useful than relying on the regular-hours paragraph further down the page.

Visit details checked September 6, 2026
Arrange playCall or text 306-220-3282 to arrange a tee time when operating on call. Source
PaymentPayment is cash or e-transfer only; the operator points to an ATM at the concession across the parking lot. Source
ChildrenAges three and under are free with a paying player. Source

Our planning take

Four paid rounds total $20 at the displayed rate. Confirm park-entry requirements separately, and arrange the golf start before travelling: an on-call course may not be staffed just because a generic daily schedule appears online.
