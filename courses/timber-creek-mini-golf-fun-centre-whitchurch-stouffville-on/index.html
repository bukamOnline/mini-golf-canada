Visiting Timber Creek Mini Golf & Fun Centre

Timber Creek has two noticeably different routes. Shipwreck is a 19-hole nautical course with bridges and steep, winding pathways. Logging Camp has 18 holes, a nature theme, more structural obstacles and shade described by the operator.

Visit details checked September 6, 2026
TimingThe operator estimates 60-75 minutes for Shipwreck. Source
EquipmentPlaying equipment is supplied; visitors may bring their own club. Source
BookingsOrdinary visits are walk-in. Groups of 20 or more must book ahead. Source

Our planning take

The choice is about terrain as well as theme. Ask specifically about Shipwreck's steep paths if mobility or stroller access matters; shade on Logging Camp does not establish an accessible route either. For a group of 20, arrange the visit rather than relying on the ordinary walk-in policy.
