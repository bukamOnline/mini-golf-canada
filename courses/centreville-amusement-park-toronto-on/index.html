Visiting Centreville Amusement Park

Centreville's miniature course is a separate ticket expense even for visitors holding an All Day Ride Pass. Players can choose nine or eighteen holes, making golf a flexible addition to an island outing rather than an included ride-pass attraction.

Visit details checked September 7, 2026
Nine or eighteenNine holes require five tickets; eighteen holes require nine tickets. Neither is included in All Day Ride Passes. Source
Where to buyIndividual ride tickets and ticket sheets are sold at the park; the park itself has no general entry charge. Source
Getting to the islandCity of Toronto ferry fares are separate from Centreville tickets and passes. Source

Our planning take

Four people playing eighteen holes need 36 tickets, compared with 20 tickets for nine holes. Compare ticket-sheet sizes against both golf and the rides you actually intend to use; do not buy day passes expecting them to cover putting. Allow separately for ferry fares and return-ferry timing when choosing the longer round.

Tickets and budget

Golf is priced in park tickets: five for nine holes or nine for eighteen holes. Ride-pass prices are not golf admission.
