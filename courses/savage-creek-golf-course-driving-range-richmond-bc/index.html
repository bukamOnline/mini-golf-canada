Visiting Savage Creek Golf Course & Driving Range

Mini Meadows is the putting course at Savage Creek, separate from its executive golf course and driving range. Its 18 holes wind past creeks, waterfalls and bridges, with the operator allowing approximately 90 minutes for a complete round.

Our planning take

Allow a full 90 minutes before sunset, plus any queue. Four players save $8 before tax by choosing a weekday instead of a weekend or holiday. The site's separate tee-time button is not needed for the walk-in putting course.
