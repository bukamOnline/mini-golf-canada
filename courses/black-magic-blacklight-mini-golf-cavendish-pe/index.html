Visiting Black Magic Blacklight Mini Golf

Black Magic is an indoor 18-hole blacklight course at 9018 Cavendish Road. Unlike many courses that price children by age, its admission bands use height, which matters when comparing tickets for siblings.

Visit details checked September 6, 2026
Height bandsThe lower paid rate applies from 36 to 57 inches tall; visitors over 57 inches pay the higher rate. Source
Small childrenChildren under 36 inches tall are free. Source

Our planning take

Measure children before budgeting rather than applying another attraction's age bands. Two players over 57 inches and two in the 36-57-inch band total $39.56 before tax. Check the Black Magic location itself when planning a multi-course Cavendish day.
