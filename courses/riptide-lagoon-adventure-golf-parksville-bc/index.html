Visiting Riptide Lagoon Adventure Golf

Riptide Lagoon separates its two courses by terrain: Lagoon winds beside the pond, while Mountain climbs upward through the attraction. The operator sells both 18- and 36-hole tickets, with senior pricing starting at 60 rather than 65.

Our planning take

For two adults and two children aged 4-12, 18 holes total $49 and 36 holes total $73 before any added tax. A ten-person party does not meet the stated 'over ten' group condition. Ask about the actual route if slopes or steps affect your course choice.
