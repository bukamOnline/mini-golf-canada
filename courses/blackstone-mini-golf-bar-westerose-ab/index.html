Visiting Blackstone Mini Golf + Bar

Blackstone pairs miniature golf at Pigeon Lake with a clubhouse kitchen, licensed bar and patio. Its child admission changes sharply at age six, so the family pass is not automatically the cheapest option for a group with younger children.

Visit details checked September 7, 2026
Child bandsAges 3-5 cost $5, ages 6-12 cost $12, and ages two and under are free. Source
Family offerThe advertised family-of-four pass is $50, with additional children at $10 and additional adults at $12. The permitted initial composition is not stated. Source
Other activityGold panning is separately listed at $7, rather than included in the golf price. Source

Our planning take

Two adults with two children aged 6-12 total $56 individually, while two adults with two children aged 3-5 total only $42. Confirm family-pass eligibility: the $50 pass may save the first family $6 but would cost the second family $8 more.
