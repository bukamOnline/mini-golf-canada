Visiting Kermesse Plus

Current mini-golf operation needs confirmation. We have not established a current operator source for the course. Contact the venue before travelling specifically for mini golf; the alternatives below offer other places to check.

Kermesse Plus rents nine- or 18-hole miniature-golf circuits as part of its event-equipment business. The operator offers indoor or outdoor installations alongside other rented games; the Saint-Cyprien address should not be treated as a walk-in attraction.
