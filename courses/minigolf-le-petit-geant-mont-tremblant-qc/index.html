Visiting Minigolf Le Petit Géant

Le Petit Geant is Tremblant's resort mini-golf option, inspired by the larger Le Geant golf course. The operator estimates 45 minutes to an hour, making it a defined activity block rather than a full-size golf booking.

Visit details checked September 6, 2026
Buying a roundThe operator says individual tickets must be purchased on site during mini-golf opening hours. Source
Extra chargesThe listed admission prices exclude both tax and the resort royalty charge. Source
BundlesMini golf can also be selected in a multi-activity package with other resort activities. Source

Our planning take

Two adults and two children aged 3-12 start at $88, before the royalty and tax. Do not present that subtotal as the checkout total. Compare a multi-activity package only when you have enough time to use its other selections.
