Visiting The Minden Experience

The Minden Experience's miniature course has a stated par of 44 and can be played independently of the kart track. The activity menu also offers golf as a small add-on to a timed kart session, which can be cheaper than buying both separately.

Visit details checked September 7, 2026
Golf aloneThe operator lists standalone Mini Putt at $8. Source
Two-activity choiceFive minutes of karting are $12 alone or $17 with either mini-putt or batting cages. Source
Three activitiesThe five-minute kart package with both golf and batting is $22; a broader $45 package uses 20-minute karting plus golf, batting and chipping. Source

Our planning take

For someone already choosing five minutes of karting, adding golf through the $17 package costs $5 rather than the $8 standalone price, a $3 difference. Choose the golf option explicitly because the same package can instead include batting. Kart-driver height requirements do not determine whether a child can play mini-putt.
