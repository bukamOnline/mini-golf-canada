Visiting Putt 'N Bounce Mini Golf

Putt 'N Bounce is an outdoor 18-hole Saskatoon course with a flower-lined route and castle, windmill and rock obstacles. Its group discount starts at ten players, with separate adult and child group rates.

Visit details checked September 6, 2026
Age splitAdult pricing begins at 13; ages 12 and under use the child rate. Source
Group pricingThe published group prices are $12 for adults and $9 for children for groups of ten or more. Source

Our planning take

A group of ten adults saves $5 before tax against individual admission, not a large percentage of the whole outing. For a small family of two adults and two children, use the ordinary $44 subtotal and ask about weather conditions before travelling.
