Visiting Mer Bleue Miniputt & Batting Cages

Mer Bleue's mini-putt is an 18-hole attraction, distinct from the property's nine-hole golf course. A same-day replay discount and a five-for-four pass make it worth choosing between one longer visit and several separate visits before paying.

Visit details checked September 6, 2026
Discount eligibilityThe reduced rate covers children aged 12 and under and seniors aged 60 and over. Source
Second roundThe operator advertises 50% off a second round on the same day. Source
Frequent-player passFive games cost $59 for adults or $51 for children and seniors, equivalent to buying four ordinary rounds. Source

Our planning take

Two adults and two children start at $55 before tax; a half-price second round adds $27.50. For repeat visits, ask whether the five-game pass is transferable and whether it expires before buying one for a family.
