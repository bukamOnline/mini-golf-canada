Visiting Chinguacousy Park

Chinguacousy Park lists mini-golf among its seasonal amenities, but the park's activities do not all share an opening timetable. Its current municipal notice flags a labour disruption and separate closures affecting some attractions, making a same-day amenity check important.

Our planning take

Check the current service notice before promising children a golf-and-paddleboat visit; a general seasonal list can lag behind an explicit closure alert. Use the municipal map to plan the course approach, then ask recreation staff about putter pickup, last play and admission rather than relying on front-desk hours.
