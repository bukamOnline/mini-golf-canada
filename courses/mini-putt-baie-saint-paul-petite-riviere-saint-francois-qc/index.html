Le Genevrier includes its on-site summer and autumn activities, including mini-golf, for chalet occupants. Day visitors have a separate admission table, making overnight-guest eligibility important when calculating a golf outing here.

Our planning take

Two adults and two children aged 14 or under save $5 with the $25 family package compared with $30 in individual visitor fees. Ask about the current mini-golf schedule and any equipment arrangements. The nearby 18-hole Golf Baie-Saint-Paul described further down the page is a different course.
