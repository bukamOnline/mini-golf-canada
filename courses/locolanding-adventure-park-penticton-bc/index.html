Visiting LocoLanding Adventure Park

LocoLanding lets visitors buy mini golf on its own or choose a multi-activity Value Pack. The pack's activities can be spread across multiple days in the year it is issued, so it does not require completing the whole park in one visit.

Visit details checked September 6, 2026
ChildrenAges three and under golf free with a paying adult; children under 12 must be accompanied by an adult. Source
Pass contentsThe Value Pack gives one turn on each included park activity, including mini golf, rather than unlimited golf replays. Source
RefundsThe published pricing is tax-inclusive and marked non-refundable. Source

Our planning take

Two regular golf admissions and two child admissions cost $42 including tax, compared with $176 for four $44 Value Packs. Start with the golf-only total, then decide which additional activities each person will actually use before buying packs.
