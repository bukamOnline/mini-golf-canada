Visiting Steele Creek Adventure Golf

The Harvest Hills Golf Centre publishes an 18-hole mini-putt at the Country Hills Boulevard address associated with this Steele Creek listing. Its age-based tickets include putters, balls and scorecards, so players do not need to add a separate equipment rental to the advertised golf price.

Our planning take

Two adults, a seven-year-old and a five-year-old have a $39 subtotal. Use the Harvest Hills Golf Centre name and published address when asking for directions. The site's opening announcement does not identify a year, so call to confirm today's play window and last equipment pickup before travelling.
