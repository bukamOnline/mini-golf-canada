Visiting Pop's Place Mini Putt and Arcade

Pop's is associated with Turkey Point's Cedar Drive beach strip, rather than an activity in central Vittoria. A public business record at 92 Cedar Drive contains 2018 visitor accounts of mini golf, arcade games and food. A neighbouring boat-rental page also uses Pop's Arcade as a Cedar Drive landmark. These sources help locate the former outing, but are not enough to promise mini-putt is operating this season.

Our planning take

If you are already visiting Turkey Point, ask locally whether the miniature course is accepting players before committing to a round. For a golf-focused trip, choose a currently confirmed alternative unless Pop's provides direct confirmation. Do not navigate to the boat operator's Port Rowan trip instructions or treat its rental prices as Pop's admission.

Tickets and budget

No current mini-putt tariff or public opening confirmation was found. Boat-rental rates and historical food purchases do not establish a golf fee.
