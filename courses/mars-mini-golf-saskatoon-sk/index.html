Visiting Mar’s Mini Golf

Mar's Mini Golf is an 18-hole, 3D glow course with an underwater-pirate theme. Paid admission includes unlimited golf rounds, while 3D glasses and spectator admission are separate listed charges.

Visit details checked September 6, 2026
Family passThe $45 family pass is for two adults and two children aged 12 and under. Source
Extra chargesSpectator entry is $3 and 3D glasses are listed at $2.50. Source
GroupsGroups of 15 or more have a posted rate of $12 per person. Source

Our planning take

Two general admissions and two child admissions total $53, so the family pass saves $8 before any tax difference. Four pairs of 3D glasses add another $10 if purchased. Reserve enough time to benefit from unlimited rounds rather than treating it as a single short game.
