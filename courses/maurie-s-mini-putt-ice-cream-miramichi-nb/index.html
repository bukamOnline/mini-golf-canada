Visiting Maurie's Mini-Putt & Ice Cream

Maurie's prices its 18-hole mini-putt equally for adults and children and publishes the tax-included checkout amounts. A second 18-hole round costs half as much as the first.

Visit details checked September 7, 2026
First roundThe operator shows $5.22 plus HST, rounded to a displayed total of $6 per person per game. Source
Second roundA second round is displayed at $3 per person including HST. Source
On-site provisionThe course page offers adult and children's insect repellent for visitors to use. Source

Our planning take

Four players pay the published $24 first-round total or $36 for two rounds each. Ask whether the second round must immediately follow the first, and leave any ice-cream purchase outside that golf total.
