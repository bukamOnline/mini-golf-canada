Visiting Mini-Putt Vanier et bar laitier Tiki Glacé

Vanier offers a choice between its classic course and Maxi-Fun, with an ice-cream counter and batting activity at the same venue. Recent publicly mirrored posts show that a tournament can affect course availability independently, an important distinction for casual visitors.

Posted pricesThe public social mirror lists classic admission at $10 adult and $9 under twelve, and Maxi-Fun at $12 per entry. These mirrored rates require confirmation with the venue. Source
Tournament exampleAn August 29, 2026 mirrored notice said Maxi-Fun would stay available to non-tournament players. This is evidence of that event arrangement, not a standing availability guarantee. Source

Our planning take

For a competition-day visit, ask which course is open to walk-ins rather than assuming the entire venue is reserved. Two adults and two under-twelves would total $38 on classic or $48 on Maxi-Fun at the mirrored prices, with tax treatment unstated. Do not use the cheaper 2023 newspaper tariff or expired name-based social promotions as current offers. Confirm batting availability separately.
