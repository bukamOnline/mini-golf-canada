Visiting The Bunker

The Bunker, formerly Putt N' Blast, has an 18-hole indoor darklight course alongside duckpin bowling and golf simulators. A round is estimated at about 40 minutes, but the last ticket is sold a full hour before closing.

Visit details checked September 6, 2026
ReservationsMini-putt is first come, first served, without advance reservations; larger groups can take longer than the estimated 40 minutes. Source
Winter footwearFrom December 1 through April 30, visitors must remove shoes to protect the carpet. Socks are available to purchase. Source
Activity add-onMini-putt is listed at $5 when added to a bowling or simulator rental, instead of the $10 standalone admission. Source

Our planning take

Bring suitable socks for a winter visit and aim to arrive before the last-ticket deadline, not just 40 minutes before closing. Four standalone rounds cost $40 before applicable tax; an add-on still requires paying for the associated lane or simulator rental.
