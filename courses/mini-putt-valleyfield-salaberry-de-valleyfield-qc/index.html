Visiting Mini-Putt Valleyfield

Mini-Putt Valleyfield sells 18-hole rounds with tax included and limits playing groups to four. Larger booked parties get a per-person discount, but still split into small groups on the course.

Visit details checked September 7, 2026
Booked groupsReservations for 12 or more reduce admission by $1 per player; groups of 20 or more have a $2 reduction from regular adult and junior prices. Source
Last departureThe last start is 30 minutes before closing. Reservations are taken by telephone during opening hours. Source
Equipment and passesOnly the venue's ball is accepted. Ten-round cards are non-transferable and valid within the current season. Source

Our planning take

Twelve adults cost $132 at the reserved group rate rather than $144 as individual tickets, but require at least three playing groups. A $90 adult ten-round card breaks even against $12 tickets during the eighth visit; buy only if enough visits remain before seasonal closure.
