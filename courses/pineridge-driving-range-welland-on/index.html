Visiting Pineridge Driving Range

Pineridge should no longer be treated as a confirmed place to play miniature golf. Its archived July 2023 announcement says the eighteen-hole mini-putt was permanently closing and a different facility would replace it. The business subsequently continued posting about the driving range, which explains why an active business listing can coexist with a closed mini-golf attraction.

Our planning take

For a family putting outing, choose another course from the nearby comparison instead of navigating to Pineridge on the strength of its old eighteen-hole listing. Range customers can still investigate range services directly. A new mini-golf opening would require its own dated announcement; the range's annual spring reopening is not that evidence.

Tickets and budget

No current mini-putt admission is offered in the reviewed material. Driving-range bucket prices would not be miniature-golf tickets.
