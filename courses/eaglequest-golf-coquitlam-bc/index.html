Visiting Eaglequest Golf

Eaglequest Coquitlam's mini-putt is an 18-hole outdoor course with landscaped grounds and bridges crossing running water. It is separate from the same facility's nine-hole par-27 golf course, whose round time and tee-booking instructions do not describe mini-putt.

Our planning take

Ask the golf shop for the mini-putt rate and last start, not a regulation-golf tee time or an eighteen-hole crossover booking. If bridge access matters, request details of the miniature course specifically. The main site's broad golf promotions and other Eaglequest locations are not evidence of a Coquitlam mini-putt discount.
