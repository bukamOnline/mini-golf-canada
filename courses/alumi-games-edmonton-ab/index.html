Visiting Alumi Games

Current mini-golf operation needs confirmation. We have not established a current operator source for the course. Contact the venue before travelling specifically for mini golf; the alternatives below offer other places to check.

Alumi Games sells portable miniature-golf sets for rental businesses, venues and events. Its nine- and 18-hole products are equipment packages with aluminium construction and lighting, not rounds available for an individual visitor to book.
