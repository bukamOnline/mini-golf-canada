Visiting Cascade Putting Course

Cascade is Blue Mountain's 18-hole, par-67 putting course at the base of the Open-Air Gondola. Its scaled-down bunkers and water hazards give it a full-size-golf style, with waterfalls and limestone landscaping around the route.

Our planning take

Wear shoes even if arriving from another summer resort activity. Compare the current resort ticket inclusions against the rest of your day's plans, and check Cascade's operating status before purchasing a pass mainly for golf. An outdoor putting ticket is not a guarantee of operation in poor conditions.
