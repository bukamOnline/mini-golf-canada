Visiting Laser Plus Sherbrooke - Laser Tag - Minigolf

Laser Plus Sherbrooke sells same-day unlimited fluorescent mini-golf with replay subject to available space. Its family ticket and golf-party package are different products, with the party including reserved room time and a limit on older participants.

Visit details checked September 7, 2026
Family admissionAges 13+ are $13.70 and children twelve and under $10.95. Two older players and two children are $41.95, before tax. Source
Replay and young childrenKeep proof of purchase for same-day replay; new visitors and reservations have priority. One child four and under is free per paying person aged thirteen or over. Source
Golf partyThe $126.10 plus-tax package covers eight players, at most two aged 13+, with a reception room for two hours. Advance reservation is required. Source

Our planning take

Two adults and two children aged five to twelve save $7.35 with the $41.95 family ticket instead of $49.30 separately. Do not count unlimited golf as uninterrupted access when busy. For a party, the included room holds fourteen but the base golf package covers only eight; extra golfers have their own charges.
