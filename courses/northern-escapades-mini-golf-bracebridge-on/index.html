Visiting Northern Escapades Mini Golf

Northern Escapades is an 18-hole outdoor course beside the Muskoka River in Bracebridge. Its snack shack serves scooped ice cream and cold treats, and the operator permits leashed pets subject to its conduct and cleanup rules.

Visit details checked September 7, 2026
Pet policyPets must remain leashed, owners must clean up, and the operator reserves the right to refuse an animal entry. Source
RefreshmentsThe snack shack offers Kawartha Dairy scooped ice cream, novelty treats, popsicles and cold drinks. Source
Group arrangementsBirthday, school, day-camp and staff outings have a separate groups-and-parties enquiry route. Source

Our planning take

Plan this as a weather-exposed riverside round, with refreshments as an optional extra. Check pet suitability before bringing an anxious or reactive dog, and use group arrangements for an organized outing rather than assuming a donation-day fundraising rate is ordinary admission.
