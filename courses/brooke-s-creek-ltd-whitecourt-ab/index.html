Visiting Brooke's Creek Ltd.

Whitecourt's chamber identifies Brooke's Creek as an outdoor 18-hole course at a seasonal campground. Mayerthorpe Public Library also lists a donated adventure-pass arrangement for mini-golf, with loan and usage conditions rather than an unrestricted free-entry offer.

Visit details checked September 7, 2026
Local confirmationThe chamber describes outdoor 18-hole mini-golf alongside laser tag and camping. Source
Pass durationThe library says adventure passes can be borrowed for one week and used once during that loan. Source
Additional guestsThe library asks families to pay admission for additional members not covered by the borrowed pass; it does not state a universal whole-family entitlement. Source

Our planning take

Ask Mayerthorpe Library about borrower eligibility, availability and exactly how many players a Brooke's Creek pass covers. Confirm the course entrance and day-visitor arrangement with the operator before travelling; a campground reservation is not established as necessary for this pass.

Tickets and budget

The sources confirm the course and library-pass arrangement, but not ordinary mini-golf admission or the pass's player allowance.
