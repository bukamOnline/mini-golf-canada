Visiting Amusement Rétro Plus

Amusement Retro Plus pairs indoor miniature golf with a 1980s-1990s atmosphere at 72 Boulevard Saint-Jean-Baptiste. The venue also has classic and modern arcade games, two escape games, a party room and a bar-restaurant.

Visit details checked September 7, 2026

Our planning take

For a mixed-age party, ask which activities and guest counts the quote covers, particularly if adding one of the two escape games. Before buying an annual membership, confirm whether golf is unlimited and whether arcade credits are included. The homepage advertises memberships without those redemption terms or a readable golf-only price.

Tickets and budget

The official page confirms golf and membership options but supplies no usable golf tariff or membership inclusions.
