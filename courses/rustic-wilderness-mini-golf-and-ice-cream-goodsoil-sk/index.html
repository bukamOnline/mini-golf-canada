Visiting Rustic Wilderness Mini Golf and Ice Cream

Goodsoil's municipal directory confirms Rustic Wilderness as an eighteen-hole mini-golf course with an ice-cream stand. It is a distinct business entry, not one of the surrounding campgrounds, building suppliers or other recreation providers on the same page.

Venue phoneThe Rustic Wilderness entry links 306-238-2112. Source

Our planning take

Ask the listed business whether both the course and ice-cream stand will be available at your arrival time. For a family outing, request the eighteen-hole total and any child bands; neither a tariff nor a season is supplied by the village. Treat the flavour count as a maximum choice rather than a promise that a particular flavour is in stock.

Tickets and budget

The municipality confirms the course and contact but does not provide a ticket price.
