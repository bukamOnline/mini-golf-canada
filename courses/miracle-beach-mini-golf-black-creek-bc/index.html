Visiting Miracle Beach Mini Golf

Miracle Beach includes equipment in its miniature-golf admission and permits non-playing chaperones to accompany children without charge. The operator estimates roughly an hour per round, with longer waits possible when other teams are already on the course.

Visit details checked September 7, 2026
SupervisionChildren and young teens must have an adult with them on the course; a chaperone who does not play is free. Source
PetsOrdinary pets are not allowed on the patio or course. They may stay in the orchard only with someone attending them; service-animal needs should be discussed with staff. Source
Booking and weatherGeneral reservations are not offered, and hours are weather-dependent. The operator directs visitors to its Facebook for closure notices. Source

Our planning take

Two adults and two children aged 4-12 total $42 before tax. If one adult only supervises, that adult need not pay the $12.75 playing rate. A pet requires someone to remain in the orchard, which changes who can join the round.
