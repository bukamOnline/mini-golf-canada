Visiting Silver Tee Driving Range

Silver Tee's mini-golf course has 18 holes and Mischief Spinners that add challenges to an ordinary scored round. Mini golf is distinct from the venue's indoor golf simulators; the mini-golf page supplies its own equipment and replay information.

Visit details checked September 6, 2026
EquipmentClub rentals are free, and balls and scorecards are available. Source
GroupsDiscounts are advertised for groups of ten or more; call 519-966-4911 for a quote. Source
Price conflictTwo tables show different first-round prices for ages four and over: $7.50 and $7. Both show a $4 same-visit replay and free play for ages three and under, but their tax statements conflict. Source

Our planning take

Ask for the current first-round total including HST before paying. Do not use the indoor simulator booking rate to budget a mini-golf visit, and confirm seasonal opening because the page also retains an older 2024 notice.

Tickets and budget

The operator displays conflicting $7 and $7.50 first-round rates and inconsistent tax wording. Confirm the payable total; both tables list a $4 same-visit replay.
