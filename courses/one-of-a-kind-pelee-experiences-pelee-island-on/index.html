Visiting One of A Kind Pelee Experiences

One of a Kind Pelee's nine-hole miniature course is on the same west-shore property as Coneheads. The golf price includes a putter, and the shop is the starting point for collecting equipment.

Visit details checked September 7, 2026
AdmissionThe operator advertises $4 per person for nine holes, including putter rental. Source
Walk-in equipmentThe detailed course section says tee-time booking is unnecessary and equipment is first come, first served. Source
Other stopsConeheads food and ice cream and the neighbouring art and gift shop are separate purchases. Source

Our planning take

Four players have a $16 golf subtotal. Stop at Coneheads before entering the course, and call ahead for a large group so equipment availability does not disrupt an island-day schedule. Confirm the day's shop hours rather than assuming rental access lasts all evening.
