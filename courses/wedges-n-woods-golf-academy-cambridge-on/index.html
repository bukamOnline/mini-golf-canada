Visiting Wedges 'N Woods Golf Academy

Wedges N Woods offers 18 miniature holes on a first-come basis, with a maximum of four players in each group. Its published prices include tax, and the Tuesday daytime discount has different rules from the ordinary same-visit replay offer.

Our planning take

Two regular players and two elementary-school-aged children total $38 including tax, or $19 during the advertised Tuesday daytime offer. Six friends must split into at least two groups. Do not assume the Tuesday discount can be halved again with the replay promotion.
