Visiting Ashbrook Golf Club

Ash Brook's Port Hope mini-golf ticket covers 18 holes. The operator lists mini golf separately from the driving range, including its own opening hours, so the golf club's general schedule is not the right cutoff for this activity.

Our planning take

Four mini-golf players can budget $40 including tax at the published rate. Do not use the range's later closing time to plan a late round; contact the club about the last mini-golf start.
