Visiting Duffer's Golf Centre

The operator website associated with Duffer's now presents Featherwind Golf Course. Its miniature-golf section describes two 18-hole courses, Pond and Creek, with a single all-you-can-play price distinct from the nine-hole regulation course.

Our planning take

Four golfers have a $20 miniature-golf subtotal. Confirm the all-you-can-play time window and whether it covers both courses on your visit. Use the Featherwind name when contacting the current operator, while this page retains the established Duffer's listing address.
