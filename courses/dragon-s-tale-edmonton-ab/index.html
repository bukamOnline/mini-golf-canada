Visiting Dragon's Tale

Dragon's Tale is West Edmonton Mall's 18-hole blacklight fantasy course. The nearby Crystal Labyrinth Mirror Maze is separately ticketed, with a lower add-on price for golf customers rather than automatic inclusion in a round.

Visit details checked September 7, 2026
Golf ticketsSingle admission is $19; ages 3-10 are $14 with a chaperone required, and seniors 55+ receive $2 off. Source
Maze decisionCrystal Labyrinth is $7 alone or a $5 add-on to Dragon's Tale admission. Source
Course rulesFinal tee time is 30 minutes before closing. Food and drinks are prohibited, only rubber putters are allowed, and the FAQ describes wheelchair access. Source

Our planning take

Two standard admissions and two children aged 3-10 have a $66 golf subtotal; adding the maze for all four raises it to $86 before any unspecified tax. Finish snacks before entering. Ask about non-playing chaperones and under-threes rather than assuming the child ticket settles every accompanying person's admission.
