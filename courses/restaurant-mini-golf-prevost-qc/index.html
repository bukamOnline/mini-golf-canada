Visiting Restaurant Mini-Golf

Current mini-golf operation needs confirmation. We have not established a current operator source for the course. Contact the venue before travelling specifically for mini golf; the alternatives below offer other places to check.

Restaurant Mini-Golf is also known locally as La Patate Bleue or Chez Paquette in Shawbridge. An April 2025 owner interview describes a longstanding Route 117 restaurant-and-putting stop, with renewed playing carpets and a treehouse added during renovations.

Visit details checked September 7, 2026
Documented courseThe local newspaper's owner interview describes an 18-hole round alongside the restaurant and ice-cream stop. Source
Family historyThe business opened in 1969; the interview traces its operation through the Paquette family and Johanne Paquette's renovation work. Source
Seasonal contextThe 2025 report describes a spring reopening and a usual pause after Halloween, not guaranteed year-round golf. Source

Our planning take

Use La Patate Bleue as an alternate name when asking locally for this course. Confirm golf availability separately from restaurant opening before making it the main outing. The interview's $5 round and cheaper Tuesday mention are historical prices, not verified current offers.
