Visiting VB's Entertainment Center / King Pinz Pub & Grill

VB's charges for mini-golf per person and per round, unlike its bowling lanes, which are charged by the hour. Weekday daytime golf has a lower price than evenings and weekends.

Visit details checked September 7, 2026
Daytime windowMonday-Friday, 11 a.m.-4 p.m., mini-golf is listed at $6.99 per person. Source
Evenings and weekendsWeekdays after 4 p.m. and Saturday-Sunday rounds are $9 per person. Source
Tuesday promotionThe venue advertises buy one round, get one free on Tuesdays; ask how the free round is redeemed. Source

Our planning take

Four players cost $27.96 in the daytime window versus $36 at the standard rate, a difference of $8.04 before unspecified tax. Do not add bowling shoe rental to a golf-only budget, and confirm whether the Tuesday offer is a replay or can be shared.
