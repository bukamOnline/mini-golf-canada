Visiting Creston Mini Golf

Creston Mini Golf offers individual admission and private event arrangements at 1531 Northwest Boulevard. The operator specifically invites advance spring and autumn event bookings, rather than presenting those seasons as unrestricted daily walk-in opening.

Our planning take

Two adults and two children aged 4-12 have a displayed subtotal of $30. For a shoulder-season birthday or school trip, obtain an agreed date and group quote before sending invitations, since private availability does not establish normal daily hours.
