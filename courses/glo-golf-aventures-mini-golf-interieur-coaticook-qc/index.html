Visiting Glo Golf Aventures Mini-golf Intérieur

Glo Golf Aventures uses a converted Coaticook church for an 18-hole indoor blacklight course with underwater, space, medieval and horror settings. Its payment policy is particularly important: cash and Interac debit are accepted, but credit cards are not.

Visit details checked September 7, 2026
Family ticketThe $42 family offer is for two adults and two children. Source
Youngest visitorsChildren aged three and under are free when accompanied by a paying adult. Source
Replay and groupsA half-price second game is offered on a regular-price entry. Out-of-hours reservations require at least 20 people; group pricing is by enquiry for 25 or more. Source

Our planning take

Two adults and two paid children cost $48 individually or $42 with the family offer, saving $6 before tax. Bring a supported payment method and do not assume the family discount stacks with half-price replay. Ask about the horror scenes before bringing someone uncomfortable with frightening imagery.
