Visiting Mini Golf

Candle Lake's municipal business directory identifies this Main Street attraction as JD's Mini Golf, with ice cream and confectionery alongside the course. The directory supplies a more specific business name and street address than the legacy generic 'Mini Golf' map label.

Visit details checked September 7, 2026

Our planning take

Use JD's name when checking directions or asking local businesses, since generic map results can be ambiguous. Confirm the last golf start and ice-cream-counter hours together before making a late stop. The directory does not say that neighbouring campground admission includes this course, so request JD's own ticket price rather than assuming a camping benefit.

Tickets and budget

The municipality confirms the business, address and contact but does not publish mini-golf admission.
