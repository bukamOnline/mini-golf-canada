Visiting Patee's Mini Putt

Patee's eighteen holes introduce Port Burwell's history through local facts and a replica lighthouse. This is a useful fit for visitors who want their golf stop to connect with the village's marine heritage, rather than treating the course as an unrelated arcade attraction.

Check-inThe course is at 32 Robinson Street, with snacks and drinks on site. Contact 519-318-6311 or pateesminiputt@gmail.com. Source
Nearby stopsThe county's itinerary pairs the course with the Marine Museum at 20 Pitt Street and the municipal beach at 1 Robinson Street. Source

Our planning take

For a history-focused outing, visit the museum before golfing so the course's local references have context, then continue to the municipal beach. The provincial park at 9 Wilson Lane is a different destination; do not confuse its admission arrangements with the village course. Confirm Patee's final tee time and any group quote directly, since tourism supplies no golf price or daily timetable.
