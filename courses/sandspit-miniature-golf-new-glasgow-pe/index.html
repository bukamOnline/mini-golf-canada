Visiting Sandspit Miniature Golf

Sandspit's 18-hole miniature course can be played without buying an amusement-park bracelet. Golf remains included in the bracelet for visitors who want the rides too, giving golf-only visitors a much smaller purchase to consider.

Visit details checked September 6, 2026
Golf-only accessA standalone round is $6.96 plus tax and does not require an online pass or reserved time. Source
Bracelet inclusionThe operator explicitly says mini golf is still included in the park bracelet. Source
StoragePay-per-use lockers are available near the ticket booth at a listed $1 per use. Source

Our planning take

Four standalone golf rounds total $27.84 before tax. Start there for a short putting stop instead of automatically buying four ride passes. If you already have the park bracelet, check its golf inclusion before paying for a second ticket.
