Visiting SkipArrr's Pirate Putt

SkipARRR's Pirate Putt is an indoor 18-hole Clifton Hill course with a ship, captain's quarters and interactive effects. Its operator estimates roughly 40 minutes for a round, extending toward an hour with larger groups or peak-time queues.

Visit details checked September 6, 2026
AccessThe operator identifies the attraction as fully accessible. Source
ChildrenSmaller putters are available; ages two and under enter free with a paying adult. Source
Age bandsChild admission covers ages 3-15, with adult admission starting at 16. Source

Our planning take

Two adults and two children aged 3-15 have a golf-only subtotal of $55.96 before tax. Compare multi-attraction passes against that starting point, and allow closer to an hour when another timed attraction follows the round.
