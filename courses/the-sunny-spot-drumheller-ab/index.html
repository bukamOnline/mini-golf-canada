Visiting The Sunny Spot

The Sunny Spot pairs an outdoor 18-hole course with a seasonal eatery. Its mini-golf terrain and structures draw on the surrounding Drumheller landscape, but the kitchen, golf and other seasonal attractions do not all follow the same operating calendar.

Visit details checked September 6, 2026
Golf startsThe operator says no golf reservation is required. Source
Food seasonThe current notice ends the full kitchen menu on September 20, 2026; ice cream and drinks remain available during subsequent mini-golf hours. Source
Other attractionThe operator says its corn maze will not operate this season while the field rests. Source

Our planning take

For an autumn outing, plan around golf with drinks or ice cream after September 20, not a guaranteed full meal. Do not rely on the July-August Wednesday burger promotion or an older corn-maze listing for a September visit.
