Visiting Mariner's Cove Miniature Golf

Mariner's Cove miniature golf plays around a large pond with sand traps and water hazards. It is available as a single attraction or in a boardwalk bundle, rather than requiring every visitor to buy admission to all neighbouring attractions.

Visit details checked September 7, 2026
Single attractionThe operator permits individual admission to each Mariner's Cove Boardwalk attraction. Source
Double PlayThe two-attraction ticket allows a choice from mini-golf, Ripley's Believe It or Not!, Wax World of the Stars and the Amazing Maze. Source
Quad PackThe four-attraction pack covers all four named attractions. Source

Our planning take

List the attractions your party genuinely wants before comparing the single, Double Play and Quad Pack prices. A two-attraction ticket is not automatically two rounds of golf; confirm its redemption rules and opening times before buying.
