Visiting Seba Beach Mini Golf & Ice Cream

Seba Beach Mini Golf combines putting with an ice-cream and food stop at 145 Fourth Street South. Its operator history traces the course from the Fahlman family to the Haakonson family, but the most important booking detail is an overlap in its current age-price table.

Visit details checked September 7, 2026
Unambiguous childrenAges 5-8 are $7 and ages four and under are free with a paying adult. Source
Teen price conflictThe table lists adults 16+ at $15 and youth 9-17 at $10, so sixteen- and seventeen-year-olds appear in both categories. Source
Seasonal scheduleThe homepage's weekday-closure note applies before the July long weekend, not a verified September schedule. Source

Our planning take

Two adults aged eighteen or older with a six-year-old and a ten-year-old total $47 at the listed age rates. Ask the operator to resolve the overlapping teen bands before budgeting for ages sixteen or seventeen. Recheck current hours instead of applying the old pre-July weekend-only notice.
