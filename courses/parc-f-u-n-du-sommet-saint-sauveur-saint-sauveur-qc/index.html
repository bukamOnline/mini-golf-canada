Visiting Parc F.U.N. du Sommet Saint-Sauveur

Parc F.U.N. sells its 18-hole mini-golf as a standalone round as well as within broader attraction passes. Visitors who only want to putt do not need to buy the Viking, Dragon and ground-ride package.

Visit details checked September 7, 2026
Single-round pricesAges 6-12 are $9.99 and ages 13+ are $15.99, shown at the same price online and on site. Source
Young childrenThe on-site table lists ages five and under free when accompanied by a paying adult. Source
Member pricingOn-site member rates are $6.99 for ages 6-12 and $9.99 for ages 13+; the required membership should be confirmed. Source

Our planning take

Two adults and two children aged 6-12 total $51.96 at ordinary golf rates before any unspecified tax. For one adult and a four-year-old, the stated on-site golf offer needs just the adult ticket. Do not buy a ride package on the assumption it includes the separately priced nighttime Odyssey experience.
