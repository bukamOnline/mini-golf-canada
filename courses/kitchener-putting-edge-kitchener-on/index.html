Visiting Kitchener Putting Edge

Kitchener Putting Edge takes reservations for its indoor glow-golf venue on King Street East, while accepting walk-ins only when space is available. Admission is required for everyone entering the course, not just the people holding a putter.

Visit details checked September 6, 2026
Payment and refundsThe venue is cashless, and online ticket sales are final. Bring a debit or credit card for an unbooked visit. Source

Our planning take

Four people entering the course cost $64 before tax, even if one only intends to accompany the others. Check the exact date and time before buying non-refundable tickets; this branch's last-start rule differs from venues that stop selling an hour before closing.
