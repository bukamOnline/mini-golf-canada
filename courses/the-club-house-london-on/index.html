Visiting The Club House

The Club House charges miniature golf by person and round, unlike its hourly simulator and duckpin-bowling products. Its published golf rate is the same throughout the week, so the other attractions' off-peak discounts should not be applied to a mini-golf budget.

Our planning take

Two golfers total $29.98 and four $59.96 before any tax or extras not specified on the page. Book the Mini Golf product, not an eight-person simulator. Early-morning and late-night offers list other activities, with no reduced miniature-golf price shown. Use 226-213-5247 to clarify the bowling add-on and your entrance.
