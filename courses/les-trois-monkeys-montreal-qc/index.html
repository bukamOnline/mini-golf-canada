Visiting Les Trois Monkeys

Lucky Putt at Les Trois Monkeys is a ten-hole indoor game scored by points, not simply by the fewest strokes. Players register their assigned putters and scan the handles at each station; speed, accuracy and trivia can all affect the automatically recorded result.

Visit details checked September 7, 2026
Group sizeA playing group can contain a maximum of six people. Source
Finding the deskLucky Putt is beyond the arcade and bowling areas, with payment and equipment available at the concierge desk. Source
Food and bookingDrinks are allowed in the Lucky Putt area but food is not. The reservation form states it is for groups of 12+, while smaller groups can walk in. Source

Our planning take

Six players cost $71.70 Monday-Thursday or $89.70 Friday-Sunday, an $18 difference before unspecified tax. A party of 12 should arrange the group booking and expect to split into at least two playing groups. Agree to use the game's points system rather than comparing the result with ordinary stroke-play scores.
