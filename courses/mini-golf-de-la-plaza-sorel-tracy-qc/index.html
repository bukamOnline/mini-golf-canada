Visiting Mini-Golf de la Plaza

Mini-Golf de la Plaza has a documented local playing community, not just a business-directory address. A March 2022 owner interview traces the course to 1990 and describes competitive and social leagues, school visits and fundraising. A public directory advertises thirty-six holes and batting machines; that is useful when asking about a visit, but it does not prove every hole or activity is available today.

Our planning take

Ask whether the current ticket buys one circuit or all available holes, and whether a league or private group affects casual play that evening. Batting-machine practice is a separate activity to ask about, not an automatic golf-ticket inclusion. The course's community history is a reason to explore organized play, but the 2022 league nights and old fundraising fees should not be used to plan a 2026 booking.

Tickets and budget

No current operator tariff was verified. Old fundraising contributions and community-outing budgets are not public admission prices.
