Visiting Minigolf du camping municipal de Péribonka

The municipal mini-golf activity in Peribonka uses the campground reception for registration. The municipality publishes a dated 2026 season, which is more useful for a September trip than assuming the course keeps summer hours indefinitely.

This profile and the other Peribonka municipal mini-golf record refer to the same course and campground check-in, not two independent destinations. Municipal source.
