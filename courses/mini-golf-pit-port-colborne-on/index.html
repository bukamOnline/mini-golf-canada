Visiting Mini golf pit

The legacy 'Mini golf pit' pin corresponds to the miniature course inside Sun Retreats Sherkston Shores. The resort confirms eighteen holes and includes unlimited mini-golf with its stays, which is different from offering unrestricted free entry to people arriving from outside the property.

Our planning take

Use the resort entrance, not the internal course pin, when navigating by car. Day visitors and guests booking through an independent cottage owner should confirm access and wristband terms for their own reservation. Do not interpret the included-golf wording as free public admission. Arcade spending and other separately charged resort activities should be budgeted in addition to the round.

Tickets and budget

Unlimited golf is advertised as included with resort stays; no standalone public-admission price or day-access guarantee is supplied.
