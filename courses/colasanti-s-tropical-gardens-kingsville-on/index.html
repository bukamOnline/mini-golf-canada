Visiting Colasanti's Tropical Gardens

Colasanti's combines an indoor 18-hole mini-golf course with a garden centre, restaurant, arcade and animal area. Its Activity Deal is explicitly a combined golf-and-animal-area ticket, useful when comparing the cost of a short round with a longer family visit.

Visit details checked September 6, 2026
Bundle contentsThe $11 Activity Deal covers one mini-golf round and one animal-area admission. Source
Activity hoursThe homepage currently lists indoor mini golf, the arcade and animal area daily from 10 a.m. to 5 p.m.; other departments have different hours. Source
PetsService animals are welcome; pets are not permitted. Source

Our planning take

Four Activity Deals total $44 before tax. Use the activity hours, not the garden centre's earlier opening, to plan a golf visit. Check outdoor habitat hours separately when the animal area is a main reason for choosing the bundle.
