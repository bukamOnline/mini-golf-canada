Visiting Rattlesnake Canyon

Rattlesnake Canyon's 18-hole course uses a mountain-and-waterfall setting with a river and pedestrian bridges. The putting route is part of a larger attraction in Osoyoos, alongside go-karts and an arcade.

Visit details checked September 6, 2026
Course routeThe operator describes bridges crossing the river beside the course's mountain backdrop. Source
Final holeThe 18th-hole centre-ring challenge offers a chance at a free golf pass. Source
Indoor breakAn arcade is located inside the mountain structure, near the go-kart entrance. Source

Our planning take

Choose this for a themed attraction stop rather than a putting-only practice session. The bridges are a reason to ask about the full playing route if steps or slopes affect your group; the source description alone does not establish wheelchair access. Confirm whether any ticket covers more than one activity.
