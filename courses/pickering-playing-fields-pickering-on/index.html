Visiting Pickering Playing Fields

Pickering Playing Fields' adventure course is recognizable by a large yellow plane in its centre. Its Old Mining Town setting includes named holes such as Miner Bill's Cabin and The Wharf, offering a more obstacle-led outing than a plain putting green.

Our planning take

Two adults and two qualifying children total $40 including tax. Confirm the children's age eligibility and the intended date before buying a package you cannot carry into another day. The range's covered bays do not shelter this adventure course, so check the operator's weather-dependent opening notice.
