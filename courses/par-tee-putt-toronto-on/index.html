Visiting Par-Tee Putt

Par-Tee Putt Toronto combines an 18-hole themed putting course with a bar, ping-pong and darts. A nine-hole ticket and an 18-hole ticket let you choose how much of the visit to devote to golf rather than the other activities.

Visit details checked September 6, 2026

Our planning take

The 18-hole ticket adds $10 per player over nine holes. For four players that is $40 more before any taxes or extras. Because this is a bar venue, confirm the age policy for your chosen time before planning a children's outing.
