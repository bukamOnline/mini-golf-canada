Visiting Tap-Ins Putting Course

Tap-Ins is a natural-grass putting course, not an obstacle-style mini-golf track. Its four-acre setting uses bentgrass greens, with the operator suggesting one to two hours for the experience. That makes it a different choice from a quick indoor round.

Our planning take

For two adults and two children aged 12 or younger, the family ticket saves $6 against four individual tickets before tax. Four rented putters add $8 before tax, so equipment can more than offset that saving.
