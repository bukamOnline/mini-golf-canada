Visiting Putters Mini Golf Course

Putters Mini Golf and Ice Cream is the putting-and-dessert stop at 4800 Marine Avenue in Powell River. Its operator offers custom gift certificates by phone throughout the year, even though the course is promoted as a summer activity.

Visit details checked September 7, 2026
At the venueThe official site confirms mini-golf, multiple ice-cream flavours and soft ice cream. Source
Gift enquiriesCustom certificates can be discussed at 604-485-7166 Monday-Friday year-round. Source
EventsThe operator promotes parties and provides admin@puttersminigolf.ca for enquiries, without publishing a package guest count or tariff. Source

Our planning take

A year-round gift-certificate phone line is not evidence that the course operates through winter. For a present, ask whether the certificate covers a fixed number of rounds or a dollar amount and whether it expires. For a party, confirm golf, ice cream and any seating separately instead of assuming the three are bundled.

Tickets and budget

The official homepage has no current mini-golf admission or party price.
