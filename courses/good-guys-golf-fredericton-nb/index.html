Visiting Good Guys Golf

Good Guys Golf has a nine-hole indoor mini-putt course alongside a full golf simulator, tabletop games and bar service. The operator welcomes all ages, and its mini-putt is priced by the round rather than by the simulator's hourly booking model.

Visit details checked September 6, 2026
ReplayThe displayed replay rate is $3.99. Source
Separate simulatorThe $40-per-hour simulator rate covers up to four players and is not a mini-putt admission price. Source
RefreshmentsThe venue advertises beverages from local producers and Covered Bridge snacks. Source

Our planning take

Two regular admissions and two under-13 admissions total $23.96 before any taxes. A short nine-hole round can work as a first visit, with the lower replay rate available if the group wants more; do not reserve the full simulator just to secure mini-putt.
