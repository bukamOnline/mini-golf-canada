Visiting Scarborough Putting Edge

Scarborough Putting Edge is a cashless mini-golf outing with bookable entry. Its location page accepts walk-ins only when space is available, making a reservation the more predictable choice when your group has a fixed arrival time.

Visit details checked September 6, 2026

Entry and final gameEveryone entering the golf course needs paid admission. The last game may start at closing time. Source

Our planning take

Budget for accompanying people who enter the course, not just those keeping score. The final-sale policy makes it worth settling the head count before paying online.
