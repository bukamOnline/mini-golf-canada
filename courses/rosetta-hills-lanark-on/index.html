Visiting Rosetta Hills

Rosetta Hills' 18 outdoor holes overlook the farm property. Mini-putt admission includes the property trails and sitting areas, but the operator explicitly excludes animal visits from that ticket.

Visit details checked September 7, 2026
What is includedGeneral mini-putt admission covers the course, property trails and sitting areas. Source
What is separateAnimal visits have their own arrangements and are not included. Source
Arrival and age bandsTickets are sold on-site with parking available. Children ten and under pay $7.99; the $9.99 rate starts over age ten. Source

Our planning take

Two adults and two children aged ten or under have a $35.96 golf subtotal before HST. A trail walk can extend the visit without buying an animal encounter, but seeing animals from the course is not the same as admission to a farm visit.
