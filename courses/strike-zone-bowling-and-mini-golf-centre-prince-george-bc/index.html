Visiting Strike Zone Bowling and Mini Golf Centre

Strike Zone's public business description identifies eighteen-hole, underwater-themed glow golf inside its bowling entertainment centre. That gives the miniature activity a distinct identity: it is advertised as a 3-D blacklight experience, not a putting green attached to a conventional golf course. The operator website could not be read during this review, so current packages remain unconfirmed.

Our planning take

When calling, ask for the eighteen-hole glow-golf price rather than a bowling-lane quote. Clarify whether any glasses associated with the advertised 3-D effect are included or optional, and whether a golf-only visit is available. People choosing a bright daytime course should note the advertised blacklight setting before committing to this venue.
