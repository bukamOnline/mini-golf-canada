Visiting Glow Mini Golf

U-Puttz's physical glow course starts with an underwater setting and moves into a dinosaur-themed back nine. Its 18 miniature holes are distinct from the separately booked golf simulators, even though both appear in the operator's retail navigation.

Our planning take

Four $8.95 golf products have a $35.80 subtotal, but confirm tax, age eligibility and redemption timing before purchase because the product text does not state them. Select the glow-golf item rather than a Pebble Beach simulator session; budget arcade credit and food separately.
