Visiting Coboconk Go-Karts & Mini-Putt

Coboconk's golf options now extend beyond one mini-putt course: the 18-hole Mischief Golf route has a spinner at each hole, while a separate billiards-style putting challenge and nine-hole chipping course can be added to the visit.

Visit details checked September 6, 2026
Billiards puttingThe putting challenge uses stripes-and-solids rules and is priced separately at $7. Source
ChippingThe nine-hole chipping challenge is a separate $15 activity, added in autumn 2025. Source
Combined ticketThe operator offers all three golf experiences for $25 per person. Source

Our planning take

The three individual golf activities total $37, so the combined $25 ticket saves $12 per person. Choose the $15 Mischief round when you only want mini golf; the additional nine chipping holes should not be counted as extra mini-putt holes.
