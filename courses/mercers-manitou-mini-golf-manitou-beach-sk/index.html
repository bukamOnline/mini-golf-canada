Visiting Mercers manitou mini golf

Mercers' Mini-Golf is the Highway 365 miniature-golf business listed by the Village of Manitou Beach. It has a different contact from Manitou Beach Golf Club, Murray Disc Golf and the regional park, all of which also appear along the same highway.

Our planning take
