Visiting FUNNECT

FUNNECT's nine-hole indoor glow course uses same-day unlimited-round admission, so a second circuit need not mean another standalone ticket. Supervising adults have no general building-entry fee, while anyone choosing to play pays for the activity.

Visit details checked September 7, 2026
Golf admissionThe FAQ gives adults $14 and children under 12 $12, plus tax, including unlimited rounds that day. Source
Time and supervisionThe operator suggests 30-45 minutes for nine holes and requires under-twelves to be supervised by a responsible adult. Source
Family bundleA $69.99 Family Pass lists unlimited golf, 40 arcade credits and an hour of duckpin bowling; the included guest count needs confirmation. Source

Our planning take

Two adult golfers and two children under twelve total $52 before tax for golf alone. The family bundle is $17.99 more, but compare it only after checking that it covers your whole group. Ask which ticket applies at age twelve, which is not assigned explicitly by the adult/under-twelve wording.
