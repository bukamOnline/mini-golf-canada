Visiting Coast To Coast Miniature Golf

Coast to Coast's 18 holes use Canadian provinces and territories as their theme. The playing surface is synthetic turf with undulating greens, offering a putting-focused round with ice-cream treats, drinks and a sitting area at the clubhouse.

Our planning take

For two adults and children aged 8 and 12, ordinary tickets total $52 before HST (2 × $14 + 2 × $12). With one child aged four and one aged eight, the subtotal is $46, not $40: the youngest child still pays $6. The Wednesday discount appears in the summer rate table, but the fall Saturday-only exception prevents planning a September weekday visit around that offer. Dusk is a closing boundary, not a published last-start time; ask the golf desk when the final group can begin.
