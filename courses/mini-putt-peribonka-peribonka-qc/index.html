Visiting Mini-Putt Péribonka

Peribonka's municipal mini-golf checks players in at the campground reception. The town publishes a specific 2026 season and tax-included tariff, making it possible to budget the round separately from the nearby free water-play facilities.

This profile and the other Peribonka municipal mini-golf record refer to the same course and campground check-in, not two independent destinations. Municipal source.

Our planning take

Two adults and two children aged six to seventeen cost $24.50 separately. The $16.50 family option would save $8 if that group qualifies, so confirm its conditions at reception. The published golf season ends September 13, 2026; do not use the pool's June-to-September description as an extension. Go to campground reception for golf registration, not the municipal-office address printed in the footer.
