Visiting Gobbles Putt For Par

Gobbles Putt For Par is a summer putting-and-treat stop in Shell Lake. The village directory gives a season from the May long weekend through the September long weekend, which matters more for an autumn visit than its listed daily hours.

Visit details checked September 7, 2026
Published seasonThe municipality lists operation from May's long weekend to September's long weekend, with 10 a.m.-10 p.m. hours during that season. Source
RefreshmentsMini-golf is listed alongside soft ice cream, slush, cotton candy and novelty items. Source
Business enquiryThe Gobbles entry gives 306-427-2278, distinct from the nearby museum's contact. Source

Our planning take

After Labour Day, phone first rather than assuming the summer 10 p.m. closing still applies. Ask whether a planned stop is for golf, refreshments or both, since the directory does not promise identical availability outside peak season. The business-licence charges elsewhere on the municipal page are not visitor admission prices.

Tickets and budget

The village directory lists a season and attractions but not a golf ticket price.
