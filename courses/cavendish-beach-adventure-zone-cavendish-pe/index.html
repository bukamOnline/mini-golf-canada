Visiting Cavendish Beach Adventure Zone

Cavendish Beach Adventure Zone offers two different 18-hole experiences: River of Adventure, recognized by its blue-lobster landmark, and Black Magic blacklight golf. Ordinary mini-golf play does not require advance booking.

Visit details checked September 7, 2026
River of AdventureThe operator describes an 18-hole route with individually designed holes and a blue lobster at arrival. Source
Black MagicThe second course has 18 glow-in-the-dark holes. Source
BookingThe operator explicitly says to show up and play, with no advance reservation required. Source

Our planning take

Choose the course style before checking ticket prices, and ask whether the selected ticket covers one route or both. A family avoiding dark environments should compare River of Adventure with Black Magic rather than treating the two as interchangeable.
