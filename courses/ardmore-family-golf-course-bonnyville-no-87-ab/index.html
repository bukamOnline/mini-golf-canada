Visiting Ardmore Family Golf Course

Ardmore Family Golf Course offers mini-golf in addition to a conventional nine-hole course. The municipality's three synthetic and six sand greens describe that larger course, not nine confirmed miniature holes.

Visit details checked September 7, 2026

Our planning take

Request a mini-golf-only quote rather than a nine-hole green fee or power-cart package. The municipality describes the overall facility as dog-friendly, but ask whether that extends to the miniature course. For a group meal, check whether catering and reserved space are part of the event quote rather than ordinary golf admission.

Tickets and budget

Neither authority source provides a miniature-golf rate; regulation-golf rental products are separate.
