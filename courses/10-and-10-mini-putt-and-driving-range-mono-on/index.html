Visiting 10 and 10 Mini Putt and Driving Range

10 and 10 combines a mini-putt course with a driving range beside its nurseries and garden centre. Its natural-grass three-hole chip-and-putt practice area belongs to the range offering, so it should not be confused with the separately priced mini-putt activity.

Visit details checked September 6, 2026
Playing groupsThe rules allow a maximum of four players per group. Source
Family ticketThe $40 family package includes two general admissions and two youth or senior admissions. Source
Range inclusionA bucket of range balls includes use of the three-hole chip-and-putt practice area, not stated admission to mini-putt. Source

Our planning take

Two general admissions and two youth or senior admissions total $44 individually, so the family package saves $4 before any tax difference. A party of six should divide into smaller playing groups under the four-player rule.
