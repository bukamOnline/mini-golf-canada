Visiting Saguenay Paintball Karting

Amusement SPK now confirms its renovated 18-hole fluorescent mini-golf course open in Chicoutimi, replacing the earlier construction notice. The updated course has themed models, a spiral slide for younger visitors and a souvenir-photo area. Bring socks, as requested on the mini-golf tariff page.

Our planning take

Two adults and children aged eight and twelve total $59 at the displayed rates (2 × $16 + 2 × $13.50). With children aged two and twelve, the subtotal becomes $45.50 because the two-year-old is free. The source does not label tax treatment or publish a family ticket, so confirm the checkout total and any group option instead of assuming a tax-inclusive package. Bring socks and ask for the final mini-golf start when planning an evening round.
