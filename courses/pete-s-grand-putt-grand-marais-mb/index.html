Visiting Pete's Grand Putt

Pete's Grand Putt connects its course to Grand Beach history through hand-built local landmarks. Reporting on its 2021 reopening describes a retro restoration by Brett and Karen Chestley, while Brett's course-specific app provides scorekeeping and a leaderboard.

Visit details checked September 7, 2026
Local obstaclesThe reopening feature describes a Highway 59 and Grand Beach sign scene, a miniature Moonlight Special train and a merry-go-round tribute to the owner's grandfather. Source
Restoration historyThe original course opened in 1978. After a proposed hotel never materialized, the restored course reopened on July 1, 2021. Source
Optional scoring appThe iPhone/iPad app lists score tracking, a leaderboard and Grand Invitational participation; its version history includes a July 2025 tournament update. Source

Our planning take

The app's free download does not mean course admission is free, and its age rating is not a golf age restriction. Ask the venue about ordinary walk-in play versus tournament dates and whether paper scorecards are available. Treat the 2021 article as background to the course's design, not a current schedule or price list.

Tickets and budget

Neither the course app listing nor the historical feature gives a current admission price.
