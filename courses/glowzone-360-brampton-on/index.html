Visiting GlowZone 360

GlowZone 360 Brampton offers an 18-hole blacklight round with ocean, forest, mountain and space scenes. The operator describes an open layout designed to let larger groups interact across holes, rather than a series of isolated rooms.

Visit details checked September 7, 2026
Golf-only admissionAges 13+ pay $10.99; children 12 and under and seniors 55+ pay $8.99, plus tax. Source
Arrival and timingThe FAQ estimates 35-45 minutes for a round and asks reserved guests to arrive 10-15 minutes early. Source
Course accessThe operator says wheelchair users can play all 18 golf holes; its separate 90% access statement concerns laser tag. Source

Our planning take

Two adults and two children have a $39.96 golf subtotal before tax. Call the Brampton branch for a tee time and check combo pricing separately: its homepage and FAQ disagree between $16.99 and $15.99. A laser-tag age recommendation should not be applied to the golf-only ticket.
