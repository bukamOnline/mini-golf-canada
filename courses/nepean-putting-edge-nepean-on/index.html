Visiting Nepean Putting Edge

The Ottawa branch is in Nepean at 5 Roydon Place. Its $16 admission applies to each person entering the course, and the branch accepts a final game starting at its closing hour rather than applying Montreal's earlier last-tee-off rule.

Visit details checked September 7, 2026
AvailabilityReservations are advised to avoid waits; a walk-in is accepted only when space is available. Source
Purchase rulesThe venue takes debit or credit rather than cash, and online purchases are final. Source

Our planning take

Two golfers and one accompanying adult need a $48 pre-tax admission budget if all three enter. Use the Ottawa/Nepean booking page and check the date-specific hours, especially after its September 7 summer-schedule end. Arrange an early group start directly instead of treating the regular calendar as flexible.
