Our planning take

Ask for the golf check-in at Lukies Store on Finlayson Street and confirm the miniature course's last start separately from shopping hours. Do not rely on the mirror's static eleven-to-six timetable: its dated posts show that hours vary. A rain-themed post is not a weather-guarantee or refund policy, so ask what happens if rain interrupts a round.
