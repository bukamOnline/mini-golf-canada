Visiting OVG Putt Lounge

OVG Putt Lounge is the summer mini-putt pop-up at Okanagan Virtual Golf in Kelowna. The operator’s current website promotes simulator leagues and its embedded updates announce the conversion back to simulator golf for winter.

Our planning take

Choose OVG for the activity currently offered, rather than relying on an old mini-putt ticket or a generic indoor label. For a physical winter round in Kelowna, compare Scandia’s year-round indoor jungle course and confirm its opening time.

Can I book winter mini-putt here?

The current operator site supports simulator golf. Check directly for any special mini-putt event or future reopening before arranging a putting outing. Source

Plan the rest of your visit

  • Scandia Golf & Games: the operator describes an 18-hole indoor course with year-round play
  • Compare Kelowna courses: separate seasonal outdoor golf, simulators and indoor putting

Tickets and budget

The previously published $10 single-round and $18 double-lap mini-putt prices were summer offers checked September 7, 2026. They are not presented as bookable winter tickets. Ask the operator about any future mini-putt dates and rates.
