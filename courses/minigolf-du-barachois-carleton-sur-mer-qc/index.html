Visiting Minigolf du Barachois

Current mini-golf operation needs confirmation. We have not established a current operator source for the course. Contact the venue before travelling specifically for mini golf; the alternatives below offer other places to check.

Carleton-sur-Mer's municipal opening report places the 18-hole Barachois mini-golf in Parc Germain-Deslauriers. Each hole is named for wildlife found in the barachois, with interpretive contributions from the regional birdwatching club.

Visit details checked September 7, 2026

Our planning take

Combine a round with learning about the species represented at the holes, but first ask the municipality where equipment is obtained and whether play is currently available. The opening article does not establish that visitors can arrive without equipment or play free at any hour.

Tickets and budget

The municipal opening report does not state current admission or equipment charges.
