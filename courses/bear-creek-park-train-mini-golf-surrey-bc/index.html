Visiting Bear Creek Park Train & Mini Golf

Bear Creek Park's 18-hole mini-golf route winds among tall trees and native landscaping. The operator estimates 30-45 minutes, with longer rounds for larger groups, and offers a separate train-and-golf combination.

Visit details checked September 6, 2026
Last entryThe final golf entry is one hour before closing. Source
Accompanying visitorsThe course does not allow spectators; everyone entering pays and plays. Source
Group eventsAn 800-square-foot deck is offered for group refreshments; call ahead to arrange the visit. Source

Our planning take

Budget for adults accompanying children on the course rather than assuming they enter free. The rate page contradicts itself about taxes: its ticket line says plus tax, while a later line says tax included. Confirm the checkout total instead of relying on either line alone.
