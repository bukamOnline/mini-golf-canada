Visiting Evergreen Golf Centre

Evergreen's miniature course has 18 holes, separate from the centre's nine-hole golf course and driving range. Its last-ticket rules are activity-specific: mini-putt stops selling a full hour before closing.

Visit details checked September 6, 2026
Last golf saleMini-putt admission is sold until one hour before closing; range sales use a different half-hour cutoff. Source
WeatherThe operator warns that extreme weather may cause early closure and asks visitors to call ahead. Source
Group eventsYouth groups and teams can book mini-putt, with food-and-drink service available by arrangement. Source

Our planning take

Use the one-hour mini-putt deadline when arranging an evening visit, not the later driving-range sales cutoff. A group expecting food should have that service included in its event arrangement rather than assuming it comes with a golf round.
