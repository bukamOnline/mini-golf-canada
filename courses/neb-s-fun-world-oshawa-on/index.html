Visiting NEB's Fun World

NEB's divides mini golf into three separate six-hole themed courses. You can play one, two or all three, making it possible to plan a shorter round without stopping partway through a single 18-hole route.

Visit details checked September 6, 2026
ThemesThe operator describes a lost city, a whimsical sea-travel course and a castle adventure. Source
Credit priceEach six-hole course is listed at 70 credits; 18-hole packages are also offered. Source
BundlesSome 18-hole packages include additional credits for arcade games or rides. Source

Our planning take

Three separate courses total 210 credits at the per-course rate. Compare that with the current 18-hole package before loading a playcard. Credits are not dollars, so the package or playcard purchase determines the cash cost; a 70-credit round should never be presented as $70.

Tickets and budget

The operator lists 70 credits per six-hole course. Confirm the cash cost of credits and current 18-hole packages; no dollar conversion is assumed.
