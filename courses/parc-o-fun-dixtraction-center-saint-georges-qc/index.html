Visiting Parc-o-fun - DIXtrAction Center

DiXtrAction's 18-hole fluorescent course follows a Greek-mythology mission through themed rooms. Video, painted scenery and three-dimensional sets accompany the golf, including Atlantis, Olympus and Greek-monster settings.

Visit details checked September 7, 2026

Our planning take

A six-person party will need two teams, so arrange adjacent starts rather than expecting everyone to play as one group. The scenery includes audiovisual storytelling as well as putting; ask about lighting or sound accommodations if these would affect a member of your party.
