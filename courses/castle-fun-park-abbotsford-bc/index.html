Visiting Castle Fun Park

Castle Fun Park's currently advertised golf attraction is Underwater Adventure Golf, an 18-hole undersea-themed course. It has its own attraction hours, which finish earlier than the general park hours shown in the website footer.

Visit details checked September 6, 2026
Course hoursThe golf page currently lists 10 a.m. to 9 p.m. daily, while the park footer closes at 10 p.m. Source
GroupsFour players are recommended; five are allowed, with larger parties encouraged to split. Source
Start and paceNo advance start booking is needed. The operator estimates about 45 minutes on busier days without imposing a playing time limit. Source
FoodOn-site concessions are available; outside food is not permitted. Source

Our planning take

Plan an evening round around the golf attraction's hours, not the later park closing. A group of eight should expect two playing groups. Two regular admissions and two children aged 4-12 total $60 before any tax treatment is confirmed.
