Visiting Conley's Driving Range & Mini Putt

Conley’s updated operator page now describes an 18-hole mini-putt course at 3420 Conley Road, with new turf, bridges and water features. This replaces its earlier coming-soon notice. The mini-putt has separate per-round prices from the driving-range buckets, and the family rate can reduce the cost of a four-player visit.

Visit details checked September 29, 2026
Admission choiceMini-putt is walk-in without tee times; the rate table charges by the round. The $13, $16 and $18 bucket prices belong to the driving range, not the miniature course. Source
Season and timetableThe FAQ describes spring through late fall operation, but its daily 9 a.m.–9 p.m. hours are expressly summer hours and can change with weather. Source
Course settingThe operator describes 18 holes with bridges, water features and freshly installed turf. These details do not establish a step-free route; ask about access to every hole when that matters. Source
Payment and foodCash, debit and major credit cards are accepted. Ice cream is on site; food trucks appear on variable schedules, so do not assume a truck is serving during the golf visit. Source

Our planning take

For two adults and children aged eight and twelve, individual tickets total $42 (2 × $12 + 2 × $9); the $35 family ticket saves $7 before tax. For one adult and two children the individual subtotal is $30, so a $35 family ticket would cost $5 more and does not match the advertised two-adult composition. Use the mini-putt tariff rather than range bucket prices, and confirm fall opening directly instead of applying summer hours to September.
