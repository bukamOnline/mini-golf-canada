Visiting Dq Mini Putt

The course commonly listed as DQ Mini Putt is now identified by its operator as Mini-Putt Hawkesbury. ASD United says it revived the local attraction and directs golfers to 80 Main Street West, behind Dairy Queen. That course entrance and mini-putt contact are more appropriate for planning a round than the restaurant address and food-service timetable.

Our planning take

Navigate to the mini-putt entrance behind the restaurant and use the ASD United number for golf questions. A Dairy Queen order is not evidence of a golf ticket or a combined meal-and-round package. Ask whether ordinary walk-in play or an organized event is operating on your date, and confirm evening lighting separately if planning a late visit.

Tickets and budget

The operator's mini-putt page does not state a regular admission price. Fundraising tournament entry fees and restaurant menu prices are not normal round prices.
