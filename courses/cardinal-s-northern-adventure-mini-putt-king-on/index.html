Visiting Cardinal's Northern Adventure Mini Putt

Northern Adventure is Cardinal's shaded outdoor 18-hole mini-putt course. Ordinary play is walk-in, with a putter and ball included, while groups of 12 or more are asked to contact the venue directly.

Visit details checked September 6, 2026
Family ticketThe posted family rate covers two adults and three children for $30 before tax. Source
Youngest playersChildren aged three and under play free with a paying guest. Source
Season noticeThe page lists a May 16, 2026 opening date but also retains a closed/reopens-in-May message. Confirm today's operation directly. Source

Our planning take

Two adults and three paying juniors would total $54.75 individually, making the stated family ticket $24.75 less before tax. For a school group, arrange the visit through the group contact rather than relying on the normal no-reservation rule.
