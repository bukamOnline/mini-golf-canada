Visiting Ure's Miniature Golf

Ure's 18-hole outdoor putting course uses breaking greens and fairways around a waterfall, two streams and four ponds. The operator limits its accessibility claim to the outside ten holes, an important distinction for anyone planning to play the full course with a mobility aid.

Visit details checked September 6, 2026
Accessible portionThe operator identifies the outside ten holes as accessible; it does not make that claim for all 18. Source
Different opening timesMini golf is listed from 9 a.m. until dusk, while the kitchen closes at 2 p.m. Source
Course designThe course was designed and built by Harris Miniature Golf, with putting breaks as well as water features. Source

Our planning take

For lunch and golf, eat before the kitchen's 2 p.m. closing or start the round early enough to finish before it. Ask staff which ten-hole route to follow if step-free access is needed and whether a partial-round rate is available.
