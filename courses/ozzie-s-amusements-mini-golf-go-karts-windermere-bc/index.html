Visiting Ozzie's Amusements - Mini Golf & Go-Karts

Columbia Valley tourism describes Ozzie's 18-hole course as a landscaped putting stop with water features. Its more detailed guide singles out a stained-glass miniature church, a rockslide tunnel and a waterwheel, giving visitors specific obstacles to look for rather than just a generic golf label.

Our planning take

Leave time for children to explore the waterwheel feature rather than planning the round as a timed race between other bookings. Confirm golf and kart operating windows separately, and check seasonal gelato service before promising it as part of the visit. The tourism sources do not establish current admission prices.

Tickets and budget

Regional tourism and the gelato supplier support the outing details; the operator website did not return readable pricing.
