Visiting Earl Haig Family Fun Park

Earl Haig sells a golf-only eighteen-hole pass as well as a combined Splash and Putt pass. Golf-only admission buys one round, not unlimited replay, and equipment requires a refundable deposit beyond the ticket cost.

Visit details checked September 7, 2026
2026 golf-only ratesAges 3-17 pay $6.61, adults 18-54 pay $8.33 and seniors 55+ pay $7.29. Additional rounds are paid separately. Source
Water-and-golf optionDaily Splash and Putt includes one golf round at $10.92 for youth, $14.57 for adults and $12.64 for seniors. Source
Deposit discrepancyThe page says $10 per item in its golf section but $5 per mini-putt item in the rental table. Confirm the amount with the park at 519-758-0963. Source

Our planning take

Two adults aged 18-54 and two children aged 3-17 total $29.88 for golf alone or $50.98 for combined water-and-golf passes, before unspecified tax and refundable deposits. Buying Splash first and then adding four $6.61 mini-putt upgrades totals $67.84, so choosing the combined pass initially saves this group $16.86. Outside food and non-alcoholic drinks are permitted.
