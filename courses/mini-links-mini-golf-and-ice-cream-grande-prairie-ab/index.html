Visiting Mini Links - Mini Golf and Ice Cream

Mini Links is a seasonal golf and ice-cream stop at the south end of Muskoseepi Park, near the Grande Prairie Museum. Ordinary rounds run first come, first served; the operator does not offer private-event rentals that close the course to other visitors.

Visit details checked September 6, 2026
Season and groupsThe stated season is May through September. Schools, camps and corporate groups should contact the operator in advance for visits during regular opening hours. Source
ReplayA second continuous round is half price; the wording does not promise a discounted return later in the day. Source
Companion accessThe operator identifies Mini Links as an Access 2 participant. Confirm the card procedure and physical route requirements when arranging a visit. Source

Our planning take

Two adults and two children aged 6-13 have a $40 first-round subtotal. Staying for a continuous second round adds $20. For younger children, calculate the published $1 per year of age instead of applying the older-child price.
