Visiting Echo Par Mini Golf

Echo-Par is an eighteen-hole mini-golf and ice-cream stop on the Pasqua Lake side of Echo Valley Provincial Park. The lake name is the useful navigation detail: Echo Valley spans a wider recreation area, and the course is not described simply as being on Echo Lake.

Visit details checked September 7, 2026
Course locationProvincial tourism places the eighteen-hole course on the shores of Pasqua Lake within Echo Valley Provincial Park. Source
Extra activitiesThe business offers kayak rentals and an ice creamery serving Chapman's hard ice cream. Source
Operator contactThe destination listing gives 306-335-7424 and links echoparminigolf.com. Source

Our planning take

Ask for Pasqua Lake-side directions and the golf opening time when calling. If combining kayaking and putting, confirm rental duration, equipment and any separate park-entry requirements before pricing the day; the tourism page does not establish a combined ticket. The operator website returned no usable details during this review, so its prices have not been inferred.

Tickets and budget

Provincial tourism confirms the activities and contact but provides no mini-golf rate.
