Visiting Par-Tici-Putt Mini Golf

PAR-TICI-PUTT belongs to Good Spirit Lake Provincial Park's recreation options, with mini-golf, soft-serve ice cream and several rental activities. Good Spirit Lake is the practical destination to use instead of the broad rural-municipality label in older map records.

Our planning take

For a park day, ask the operator to separate your golf total from timed bicycle or paddling rentals. Verify the park entrance and whether any vehicle-entry fee applies before using the rural municipality as your navigation destination. Confirm rental return times first if you want a golf round afterward; this source does not publish those cutoffs.
