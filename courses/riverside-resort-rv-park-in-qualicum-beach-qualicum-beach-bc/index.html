Visiting Riverside Resort & RV Park in Qualicum Beach

Riverside Ridge is the 18-hole mini-golf course at Riverside Resort in Qualicum Beach. The resort explicitly admits the public to the course, so an overnight booking is not required for the listed day-visitor tickets.

Visit details checked September 6, 2026
Resort guestsThe resort lists mini golf as complimentary for its guests. Source
Family admissionThe golf-only family package covers up to five people. A separate family golf-and-pool package is also offered. Source
After the roundThe property has a playground and a convenience store selling refreshments. Source

Our planning take

Two adults and two children aged 12 or under total $44 before tax individually, $1 less than the $45 family ticket. The family package becomes more attractive with a fifth player. Check pool operation separately before buying the combined ticket.
