Visiting Professor WEM's Adventure Golf

Professor WEM's Adventure Golf is West Edmonton Mall's 18-hole course with its own green fees and last-tee-time rule. It is a separate attraction from Dragon's Tale; a birthday package or multi-park pass should not be mistaken for the ordinary single-player ticket.

Visit details checked September 7, 2026
Child admissionAges 3-10 pay $14 and require a chaperone. General admission starts at age 11. Source
Senior reductionThe ticket page advertises $2 off for visitors aged 55 and over. Source
Last startFinal tee time is 30 minutes before closing. Source

Our planning take

Two players aged 11+ and two children aged 3-10 total $66 before any unspecified tax. Leave the half-hour entry margin when planning around shopping or meals, and check whether a chaperone who does not play needs a ticket.
