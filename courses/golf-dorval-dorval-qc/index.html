Visiting Golf Dorval

Golf Dorval's miniature course is an 18-hole, par-56 layout operated by Bistro 19. Several par-three and par-four holes make the published 75-90 minute playing estimate more useful than assuming a quick half-hour circuit.

Visit details checked September 7, 2026
Golf-only tariffRegular admission is $16.75; seniors and children twelve and under are $13.75, all plus tax. Source
Arrival and groupsIndividual play is first come, first served without reservations. Groups of twenty or more have a dedicated reservation form. Source
Practical contactNo mini-golf dress code applies. The mini-golf information line is 438-998-0648, with enquiries listed from noon to 5 p.m. Source

Our planning take

Two regular-price adults and two children twelve and under total $61 before tax. Leave at least the operator's 75-90 minute play estimate, plus any queue, before another booking. Use the miniature-golf line rather than a regulation-golf tee-time reservation, and ask the senior qualifying age because the tariff does not specify it.
