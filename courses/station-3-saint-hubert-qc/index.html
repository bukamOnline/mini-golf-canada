Visiting Station 3

Station 3 pairs indoor mini-golf with a snack counter and dairy bar, but its golf, food and ice-cream closing times are different. Group tickets can materially lower the cost compared with buying every player a $16 individual admission.

Visit details checked September 7, 2026
Group-sized ticketsAges three and over pay $16 individually; three players are $42, four are $56 and five are $64, all before tax. Source
ReservationsWalk-ins are allowed. Online bookings require at least two people; groups larger than four use consecutive starts, with more than 20 arranged directly. Source
What to bringThe operator requests clean, dry shoes in winter and rainy weather. The published summer final start is 9:15 p.m., ahead of the 9:30 snack-counter and 10 p.m. dairy-bar closes. Source

Our planning take

Five players save $16 with the $64 package versus five individual tickets. A four-person food combo costs $92, or $36 more than the golf-only quartet; compare that increment with the included meal rather than the individual ticket price. Party seating is in the dining room, not a separate reception room.
