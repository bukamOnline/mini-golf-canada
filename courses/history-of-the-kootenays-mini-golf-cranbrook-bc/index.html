Visiting History of the Kootenays Mini Golf

Elizabeth Lake Lodge's History of the Kootenays course is open to the public, with 18 holes and a par of 70. The operator gives an explicit 2026 season of April 18-October 30; an overnight lodge reservation is not required to play.

Visit details checked September 7, 2026
Walk-in playNo mini-golf reservation is needed; the operator advertises 9 a.m. until dark during the stated season. Source
PaymentDebit and credit cards are accepted, and a cash discount is mentioned without its amount. Source
Conflicting youth cutoffTwo tables disagree on whether the $9 youth rate ends at age 19 or 23. Both give adults $10, paid children 12 and under $8, and ages three and under free. Source

Our planning take

Two adults and two children aged 4-12 have a consistent published subtotal of $36. Ask specifically about ages 20-23 and senior eligibility rather than choosing one of the conflicting tables. The concession's September schedule ends earlier than the golf season, so plan refreshments separately on later autumn visits.
