Visiting Wizards' Golf

Wizards' Golf is an indoor, climate-controlled 18-hole glow course near the top of Clifton Hill, beside Ripley's Believe It or Not. The operator describes the two-storey course as both wheelchair accessible and wheelchair playable, a more specific claim than entrance access alone.

Visit details checked September 6, 2026
Non-playing visitorsA separate non-golfer ticket is available for people who want to accompany players or take pictures. Source
PurchasingIndividual tickets are sold in person; the broader Clifton Hill Fun Pass is available online. Source
SeasonThe operator describes year-round play, seven days a week. Source

Our planning take

Two adults and two children aged 3-12 cost $35.96 before HST for golf alone. Compare that with a multi-attraction pass only after deciding which other attractions you will use; a non-playing companion can use the lower spectator ticket instead of a full round.
