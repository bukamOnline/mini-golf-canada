Visiting Sunnidell Golf Course

Sunnidell's 2026 rate sheet makes miniature golf a separate low-cost activity from its full-size course and virtual golf bays. The $6 adult mini-putt fee should not be confused with an 18-hole green fee or the hourly simulator charge.

Visit details checked September 7, 2026

Rental distinctionThe $10 club rental appears in the general golf rental section, not as a stated mini-putt equipment surcharge. Source
Booking distinctionThe stated tee-time requirement applies to virtual golf; it does not establish that miniature golf needs a Chronogolf reservation. Source

Our planning take

Two adults with children aged four and eight have a $16 tax-inclusive mini-putt total. Ask the clubhouse for the upper child-age cutoff and putter pickup details. Select mini-putt explicitly when calling 613-498-0775 so a request for 'golf' is not interpreted as the full-size course or simulator.
