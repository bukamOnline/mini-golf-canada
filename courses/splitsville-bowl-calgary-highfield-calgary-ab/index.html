Visiting Splitsville Bowl Calgary Highfield

Highfield's glow-in-the-dark mini-golf course is a nine-hole activity inside Splitsville on 42 Avenue SE. The same centre also has golf simulators, but those are a separate experience from walking and putting through the mini-golf course.

Visit details checked September 6, 2026
Other activitiesThe centre has 30 bowling lanes, including eight VIP lanes, plus an arcade. Source
ChildrenThe operator describes mini golf as family-friendly but directs visitors to its admission policy for late-night age restrictions. Source
FoodThe kitchen offers kids' meals alongside a wider food menu and licensed bar. Source

Our planning take

Choose this nine-hole format when golf is one part of a multi-activity visit. For an evening with children, check admission restrictions first; do not book a simulator bay or a bowling promotion assuming either is a mini-golf ticket.

Tickets and budget

Bowling and simulator prices are separate from the nine-hole glow course. The reviewed location page does not state a dedicated mini-golf price.
