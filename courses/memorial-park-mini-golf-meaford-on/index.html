Visiting Memorial Park Mini-Golf

Meaford's Memorial Park mini-golf is open to the public as well as campers, with 18 holes and putter rental included. Parking is a separate part of the cost for nonresidents, rather than automatically free with golf admission.

Visit details checked September 7, 2026
Golf admissionThe current municipal page lists $6 per person per round including putter rental. Source
Nonresident parkingPaid parking is $10 per day, in effect May 1-October 31 from 10 a.m.-6 p.m., seven days a week. Source
Resident registrationResidents must register their vehicle for the current season to obtain free parking; residency alone does not complete registration. Source

Our planning take

Four golfers cost $24 for the round, or $34 including one car's advertised nonresident day parking during paid hours. Complete parking payment through the official HONK route and confirm the course's equipment-desk hours; park access does not establish 24-hour golf.
