Visiting Harry Putter Golf Academy

Harry Putter's 18-hole miniature course is at the Islington Avenue and Rutherford Road golf centre. Mini-golf visitors do not need to book a round, unlike arrangements they might make for instruction or a larger organized event.

Our planning take

A family of two adults and two children aged 12 or under has a $44 golf subtotal. Keep driving-range ball buckets and the separately listed range-club rental out of that calculation; they are not the mini-golf ticket.
