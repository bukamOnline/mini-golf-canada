Visiting Crickle Creek

Crickle Creek's renovated 18-hole course offers two targets at each hole: blue pins for regular play and yellow pins for the harder Champion option. Mini golf has its own admission and seasonal schedule, separate from the Fun Zone and the property's pitch-and-putt course.

Visit details checked September 6, 2026

Wristband add-onMini golf can be added to any colour attraction wristband for $6; standalone adult admission is $12. Source
Autumn accessSeptember-October hours are weekends 11 a.m.-6 p.m., with closure after the Thanksgiving long weekend. Source

Our planning take

For two adults and two children aged 4-12, standalone golf totals $44. The $6 add-on saves money only when the group already needs the other attraction wristbands. Agree on regular or Champion targets before scoring a competitive family round.
