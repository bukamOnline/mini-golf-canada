Visiting Hooplas Mini Golf and Ice Cream

Hooplas is an 18-hole river-valley course with flowing-water features and a soft-serve stop. Its child prices have two paid age bands, so a family with a six-year-old and a ten-year-old should not budget the same rate for both.

Visit details checked September 6, 2026
Age bandsAges 4-7 pay $6 and ages 8-11 pay $8; the $10 rate begins at age 12. Source
Youngest playersThe admission table lists ages 1-3 as free. Source
Seasonal scheduleThe current posted schedule opens at 4 p.m. on weekdays and noon on weekends, weather permitting. Source

Our planning take

Two adults with children aged six and ten have a $34 golf subtotal. Treat soft serve as a separate purchase. Check the weather and current schedule before an afternoon weekday trip, when the displayed opening time is later than on weekends.
