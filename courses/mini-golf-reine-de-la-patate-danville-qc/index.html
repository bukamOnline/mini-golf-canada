Visiting Mini Golf - Reine de la Patate

La Reine de la Patate combines a cantine stop with miniature golf in Danville. Regional tourism specifically pairs a round with ice cream, while the local Espace du Carmel venue also identifies a children's play area at the cantine.

Our planning take

For a short family stop, check golf and food service at the cantine together. A meal receipt is not evidence that golf is included: neither source gives that condition or a ticket price. Burbank can provide a separate nature stop, but do not treat its paths as part of the course or assume a particular accessible connection from the restaurant.

Tickets and budget

Local sources confirm the golf-and-cantine pairing but not a numerical admission charge or meal bundle.
