Visiting Putting Edge Spheretech

Spheretech's Saint-Laurent course is in Suite 140 at 3500 Boulevard de la Cote-Vertu. Sunday has a distinctly earlier closing time than Friday and Saturday in both its regular and summer timetables, with final tee-off another hour before closing.

Our planning take

Four entrants total $74.32 at the published inclusive rate. Check the live Sunday timetable before choosing dinner first: this branch's Sunday round cannot be planned using Centre-Ville's later closing schedule. Select Spheretech explicitly when buying either admission or a gift card.
