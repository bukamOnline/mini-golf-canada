Visiting Vibez Mini Golf

Vibez offers an indoor 18-hole course and a separate Pool & Putt game at its Fraser Highway venue. The operator describes year-round play and accepts both reservations and walk-ins.

Our planning take

Check whether your selected booking is for the standard course, Pool & Putt or a combination before paying. Reserve when your group needs a fixed time, and use the unit number to distinguish the entrance from other businesses along Fraser Highway.
