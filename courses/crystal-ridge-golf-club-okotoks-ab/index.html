Visiting Crystal Ridge Golf Club

Crystal Ridge's outdoor mini-golf accepts visitors without a tee time, despite the full-size golf booking button elsewhere on its website. Its family ticket covers two adults and two children, while the youngest children are charged only if participating.

Visit details checked September 7, 2026
Family ticketTwo adults and two kids are listed at $32. Source
ChildrenAges 5-10 are $7 and must be accompanied by an adult; under-fives who play are $5. Source
Food on the courseThe operator permits its grab-and-go food and beverages during mini-golf, including a selection of alcoholic drinks. Source

Our planning take

Two adults with two children aged 5-10 total $38 individually, so the listed family ticket saves $6. With two participating under-fives the individual subtotal is $34, making the difference only $2. Confirm the unspecified youth age band and family eligibility before choosing a ticket.
