Visiting Buccaneer's Cove Mini Golf

Buccaneers' Cove has its own activity page on Gage Golf & Curling Club's website, with a mini-golf contact and a Friday evening replay offer. That is more useful than using the main golf club's tee-time arrangements: the miniature course advertises daytime play, moonlight golf and separate private bookings.

Our planning take

Choose Friday moonlight play when repeat rounds are the attraction; ask for the evening ticket price rather than assuming the daytime rate includes it. For a daytime stop, confirm the last start with the mini-golf contact, particularly after rain or near the end of the season. Groups should request miniature-golf availability directly instead of reserving a conventional golf tee time.

Tickets and budget

The operator describes Friday unlimited-round play but does not publish a numeric mini-golf fee on the reviewed page.
