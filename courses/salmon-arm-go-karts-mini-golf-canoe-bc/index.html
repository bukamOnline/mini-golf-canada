Visiting Salmon Arm Go-Karts & Mini-Golf

Salmon Arm Go-Karts' miniature course has 18 holes themed around Wonders of the World. Golf and karting are separate activities, and the operator's driving-height and licence restrictions describe the karts rather than the putting course.

Visit details checked September 30, 2026

Karting distinctionChildren below the stated driving height can ride as passengers with an adult in a double kart; this is not a mini-golf participation rule. Source
Season wordingThe operator page retains a closed-for-2025 notice and describes the planned 2026 season as May to September; it gives no day-specific 2026 autumn timetable. Source

Our planning take

A group with children too small to drive can still enquire about the miniature course independently. Compare a golf-only purchase with a combined outing only after confirming kart eligibility and the separate costs, and budget concession purchases outside the round.
