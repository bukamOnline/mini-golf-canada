Visiting Northfield Golf Centre

Northfield's 18-hole mini-putt is floodlit for evening play. It advertises a two-for-one Tuesday promotion, but holidays are excluded and the last-entry rule still applies.

Visit details checked September 7, 2026
Tuesday exceptionThe two-for-one offer is advertised all day on Tuesdays, excluding holidays. Source
Last callThe homepage gives last call for mini-golf as half an hour before closing. Source
Evening settingThe mini-putt page explicitly describes floodlighting after sunset. Source

Our planning take

A Tuesday can be worth comparing for a two-person visit, but confirm the base ticket price and whether the offer covers two players or a replay. Floodlighting does not mean late arrivals are accepted after the half-hour cutoff.
