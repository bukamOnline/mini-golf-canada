Visiting The Putting Zone

The Putting Zone is listed at Tennis Courts, 2801 67 Ave, Lloydminster, AB T9V 3K3.

  • The City of Lloydminster program guide lists The Putting Zone as a mini-golf amenity in Bud Miller All Seasons Park.
  • Municipal planning material describes an 18-hole outdoor course and identifies it as Lloydminster's only mini-golf course at publication.
