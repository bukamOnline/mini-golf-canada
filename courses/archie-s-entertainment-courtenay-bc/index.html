Visiting Archie's Entertainment

Archie's combines glow mini-putt, arcade games and Bazooka Ball near Walmart in Courtenay. Ordinary mini-putt is walk-in, while birthdays and group events need advance booking; those are different entry arrangements, not interchangeable ticket types.

Visit details checked September 6, 2026
Party sizeBirthday packages include ten guest places, counting the guest of honour. Source
Accompanying adultsAdults may stay with children but need their own ticket to play mini-putt or Bazooka Ball. Source
Food rulesOutside food and cake are allowed for booked party spaces only. The venue describes itself as nut-free and asks visitors with allergy questions to contact it. Source

Our planning take

For a birthday, count the birthday child within the ten included places and budget separately for adults who want to play. Discuss specific allergies directly rather than treating the nut-free description as a guarantee of no cross-contact.
