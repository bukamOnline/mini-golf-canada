Visiting Target Golf Centre

Target Golf combines eighteen miniature-golf holes with an outdoor play structure, patio and music at 4870 Bank Street. It can suit a mixed-activity outing, but the driving range's novelty targets and the batting cages are separate from the mini-golf layout.

Visit details checked September 7, 2026
Golf distinctionThe operator lists miniature golf separately from its practice putting green, sand bunker and 25 hitting stations. Source
Other activitiesTwo batting cages offer selectable speeds and softball or hardball; a police car and scooter target belong to the driving range. Source
Direct contactMini-golf enquiries can go to 613-266-1948 or info@targetgolf.ca. Source

Our planning take

For a group splitting between golf and batting, first agree on the activities and ask whether tickets are separate. Do not expect the siren targets to be obstacles on the miniature course. An old customer account of a weather-related voucher is not a published refund or rain-check policy; confirm that directly when conditions are uncertain.
