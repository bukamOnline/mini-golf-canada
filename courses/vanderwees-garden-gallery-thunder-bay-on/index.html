Why the normal listing is withheld

Mini Golf Canada requires enough information to identify a public mini golf activity and create a useful planning profile. A business name, map category or old reference alone cannot establish that visitors can currently play. Crawl failures are treated as unknown rather than as closures, and an official source that describes a different business type takes priority over older profile history.

Normal profiles provide source-backed course facts, dated hours, admission guidance, public-feedback context, nearby comparisons and a clear limitations statement. Publishing a full page without the evidence to support those sections would add search inventory without helping a visitor make a reliable decision.

How to verify a current course

Look for a venue-controlled page that explicitly describes mini golf at the published address. Municipal recreation pages, current tourism listings and direct operator information can also resolve identity. Check the operating season, exact activity, phone and address together because parent attractions and named course areas are sometimes exported as separate records.

Send a source or correction

Operators and visitors can submit a current source for review. Include the exact business name, address, public-course description and the relationship between any parent venue and attraction name. A verified update can restore a maintained profile without changing this URL.
