Visiting Au 19e - Mini-golf & Terrasse

Au 19e has an 18-hole outdoor rooftop course at Complexe Aloha, with sloping greens, sand and water features. Its updated visitor page sells a day of repeat play, while the fall schedule limits the food portion of the outing: the operator says the kitchen is closed for the rest of the season.

Visit details checked September 29, 2026
Hours and arrivalThe operator publishes July–October opening every day from 1 to 9 p.m., subject to weather, with possible later closing when busy. No reservation is required. This schedule is not a last-entry promise. Source
Replay boundaryAfter completing 18 holes, players may start again without paying again. The day ticket ends when you leave the premises or return the putter; it is not a come-and-go day pass. Source
Fall food limitationThe homepage says the kitchen is closed for the rest of the season, although its general marketing still describes meals. Plan food separately from golf until that notice changes. Source
Family and group billingThe family package covers two adults and two children, or one adult and three children. Family and group packages must be paid on one invoice. The homepage gives two to six players per group, so larger bookings need multiple playing groups. Source
Scoring and timeThe operator advertises a digital scorecard and an average 90-minute experience. Confirm an alternative if your party cannot use a phone; the duration is not a guaranteed finish time. Source

Our planning take

For two adults and children aged 8 and 12 during the dated offer, individual tickets total $59.84 (2 × $18.71 + 2 × $11.21), while the $44.96 family package saves $14.88 before taxes. For two adults and a child aged four, individual admission is $37.42 because the accompanied child is free; the family package costs $7.54 more. Buy by the actual age mix, keep the party on one invoice for a package, and finish repeat rounds before returning equipment. Food and rooftop access conditions need separate checks.
