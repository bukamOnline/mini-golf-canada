Visiting Au 19e - Mini-golf & Terrasse

Au 19e describes an 18-hole rooftop course at Complexe Aloha, with undulating greens, sand and water features inspired by full-size golf. Its ticket and digital-scorecard instructions are published, but daily opening times are still marked as coming soon on the operator's visit page. Confirm admission before planning a rooftop outing.

Our planning take

Two adults and two children aged 12 or under have a $79.80 subtotal before taxes. Have a phone available for the scorecard and ask for another scoring option if you do not want an emailed result. Check weather and rooftop access before committing to an outdoor visit.
