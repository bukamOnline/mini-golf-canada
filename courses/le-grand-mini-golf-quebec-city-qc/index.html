Visiting Le Grand Mini-golf

Le Grand Mini-golf combines 15 indoor putting holes with three mini-hockey holes on synthetic ice. The route uses Quebec history and hockey as its theme, so it is a mixed 18-station experience rather than 18 conventional mini-golf holes.

Visit details checked September 6, 2026
TimeThe operator recommends allowing approximately 90 minutes to two hours. Source
Young playersThe course is described as suitable from around ages four to five with an adult; younger children can use some free-play areas under parental supervision. Source
ArrivalWalk-ins are possible, with online reservations recommended. The operator provides a map of nearby paid and free parking options. Source

Our planning take

Allow time for the change from putting to mini hockey when arranging dinner or parking. Tell the group it is a mixed-activity course before booking, especially if someone expects a conventional 18-hole scorecard. Use the parking map instead of assuming the venue has its own free lot.
