Visiting 1000 Island Golf Centre

Brockville Tourism identifies an 18-hole miniature course at 1000 Island Golf Centre, alongside a driving range and virtual golf. The virtual experience provides the year-round option; that does not establish winter opening or indoor play for the miniature course.

Our planning take

Ask specifically for miniature-golf opening and pricing when calling, particularly outside summer. A street-level entrance is useful information but is not enough to plan a full wheelchair round without confirming the course route. Do not navigate to the tourism office address in the source footer; it is not the golf centre.

Tickets and budget

The tourism listing confirms the attraction but supplies no miniature-golf tariff.
