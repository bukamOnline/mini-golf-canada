Visiting Granary Road - Park, Mini Golf & Family Attraction

YesterYear Mini Golf at Granary Road is an 18-green, par-72 course. Its published playing rules limit group size and strokes, so large families and event organizers can plan the round's structure before arrival.

Our planning take

A party of eight should plan two playing groups, not one large team. Agree on the six-stroke cap before beginning so beginners can move on without delaying the group behind. Check whether your chosen Granary Road ticket includes the activities beyond golf.
