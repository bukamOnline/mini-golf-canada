Visiting Hewitts Fun Farm

Hewitt's sells miniature golf by itself, as a season pass, or as an inexpensive upgrade to Fun Farm admission. Golf and farm tickets are bought at the Fun Farm entrance, whose entry cutoff is earlier than both closing time and some farm-market hours.

Visit details checked September 7, 2026
Golf-only choicesOne round costs $7.53 per person plus tax; a miniature-golf season pass is $50 plus tax. Source
Farm upgradePublished summer and Pumpkinfest ticket pairs each add $2 before tax for the version including mini-golf. Source
Entry cutoffNo Fun Farm entry is allowed after 4 p.m. The page lists Thursday-Monday farm operation, distinct from the shop's longer schedule. Source

Our planning take

If you already intend to visit the farm, buy the combined ticket: its $2 golf increment is $5.53 less than adding a separate $7.53 round. At standard standalone prices, seven rounds cost $52.71 before tax, making the $50 pass worth investigating for repeat visits; confirm validity and replay rules first. Groups of twenty or more should email hewittsfunfarm@hotmail.com.
