Visiting Rigolfeur Ste Julie

Complexe Julie-Quilles has two different putting experiences: Rigolfeur with surprise effects and a traditional 18-hole Mini-Putt in the RDS style. Choosing between them changes the play style, the price and the published summer schedule.

Traditional alternativeThe separate 18-hole Mini-Putt costs $11 for ages 13+ and $8 for children 12 and under. Source
Group thresholdBoth pages publish lower rates for groups of 20 or more and use 450-649-6668 for arrangements. Source

Our planning take

Two adults and two children cost $44 for Rigolfeur or $38 for traditional Mini-Putt before unspecified tax, a $6 difference. If anyone wants to avoid sudden sounds or water effects, ask for the traditional course specifically. Check its earlier Sunday-to-Tuesday summer closing rather than using Rigolfeur's longer hours.
