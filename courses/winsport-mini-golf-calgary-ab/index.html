Visiting WinSport Mini Golf

WinSport advertises an 18-hole outdoor course and a separate Haunted Mini Golf programme for autumn 2026. The activity page supplies event dates and a booking link even when the site-wide status widget says Closed; that widget describes a moment in time, not a permanent closure.

Our planning take

For an October outing, choose a listed event session before planning arrival, then allow time to check in before the last start. A closed status widget outside a session is not enough to rule out a later booking. If stairs or bridges are a barrier, settle the route with guest services before buying. Compare the event checkout total with other Calgary courses rather than using the ordinary green-fee table.

Tickets and budget

Haunted Mini Golf is excluded from the regular green-fee table. Check the event booking price for your date and party.
