Visiting Arbortown Mini-golf

Arbortown Mini-golf has 18 holes across from the Arbor and about two blocks from Port Dover's beach. Its Main Street location makes it a potential stop on a walking visit to the waterfront, rather than a separate out-of-town golf excursion.
