Visiting PROJCT Golf Experience

Current mini-golf operation needs confirmation. We have not established a current operator source for the course. Contact the venue before travelling specifically for mini golf; the alternatives below offer other places to check.

PROJCT Golf Experience supplies portable mini-golf for organized events. Customers can choose three-, six- or nine-hole configurations, with the layout sized to the event space and crowd rather than a fixed public course.

Visit details checked September 6, 2026
Price unitThe advertised $1,449 nine-hole experience is an event offering, not a per-person round. Source
FormatThe operator describes portable turf holes and obstacles for corporate events, festivals and other organized gatherings. Source

Our planning take

Request a written quote covering your date, site dimensions, setup and collection, staffing and taxes. Do not travel to the business address expecting drop-in golf; the service is arranged for an event location.

Tickets and budget

The nine-hole mobile experience is advertised at $1,449; confirm the event scope, taxes and delivery. This is not per-player admission.
