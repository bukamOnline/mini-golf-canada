Visiting Par-Tee Putt

Par-Tee Putt Vancouver is an 18-hole mini-golf bar with city-specific holes and drinks permitted while putting. Family access ends earlier on Fridays and Saturdays than on the rest of the week, so the late-night programme is not a children's admission offer.

Our planning take

Reserve a specific tee time if bringing children and finish before the appropriate guardian-accompanied cutoff. Two standard advertised tickets total $49.90 before unspecified tax or checkout fees. The homepage contradicts itself on date-night weekdays, so ask about that promotion rather than building an evening around one banner.
