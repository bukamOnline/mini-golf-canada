Visiting The Whole Scoop Ice Cream & Mini Golf

The Whole Scoop's custom-built 18-hole course is at its Stony Plain flagship, not every ice-cream shop in the chain. Its current 2026 notice sets mini-golf last call at 7 p.m. from September 1, earlier than the shop's stated closing time.

Our planning take

Two adults with children aged ten and twelve total $49 at the listed rates, versus $44 with two children in the 3-10 band. Arrive before the 7 p.m. golf cutoff rather than the ice-cream closing time, and plan a meal elsewhere if you were relying on the separately closed grill.
