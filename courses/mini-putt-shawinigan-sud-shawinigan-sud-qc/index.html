Visiting Mini-Putt Shawinigan-Sud

Mini-Putt Shawinigan-Sud's 2026 tariff offers eighteen or thirty-six holes, with the longer option costing just one dollar more in every published age band. Its food-and-golf special is another distinct option, not an automatic inclusion with a golf ticket.

Visit details checked September 7, 2026
Eighteen holesThe official rate image lists adults $8.50, ages 9-17 and seniors sixty-five-plus $6, and children eight and under $2. Source
Thirty-six holesThe corresponding rates are $9.50, $7 and $3. The $1 discount coupon excludes children eight and under and cannot combine with another promotion. Source
Food packageThe poster advertises a sandwich, submarine or pizza with a canned drink, chips and eighteen holes for $13, or thirty-six holes for $14. Source

Our planning take

Two adults with a seven-year-old and a twelve-year-old cost $25 for eighteen holes or $29 for thirty-six, before any unspecified tax. Decide on the longer round before paying and allow more time. Bring cash: the site's payment notice says cash only. The posted September schedule differs from July and August, so do not assume midnight closing.
