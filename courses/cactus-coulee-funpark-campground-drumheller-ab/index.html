Visiting Cactus Coulee FunPark & Campground

Cactus Coulee's eighteen-hole miniature course is part of a Badlands recreation stop with go-karts, EuroBungy and camping. The operator's course photograph shows a low-bordered putting layout in an exposed outdoor landscape, rather than an indoor glow attraction.

Our planning take

Ask for an eighteen-hole golf-only price and whether any camper discount applies to golf before including one in your budget. The full-face helmet instruction on the homepage applies to Bobcat kart drivers, not miniature golfers. For the exposed course shown in the official photo, check weather and bring suitable sun protection rather than planning around indoor shelter.

Tickets and budget

The official homepage confirms eighteen holes but does not state a golf tariff.
