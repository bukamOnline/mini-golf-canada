Cookie Policy

Cookie categories, browser storage and privacy controls used by Mini Golf Canada.

Effective date: July 28, 2026

This policy supplements the privacy policy. Cookies are small browser files, and similar technologies include local storage, tags and device identifiers.

Necessary storage

The site may use browser storage for security, reliable operation and remembering an explicit privacy choice. A temporary session value records only whether the visitor is in a region that requires the consent prompt. These values do not identify a person.

Analytics

Google Analytics helps measure page use and directory interactions. The Google tag runs in advanced, region-aware Consent Mode. Standard analytics measurement operates outside the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, Switzerland and Quebec unless the visitor declines analytics cookies. In those restricted regions, analytics storage is denied by default, a consent prompt is shown, and Google may receive cookieless measurement signals before a choice. Declining analytics does not prevent access to listings, search or location sorting.

The regional prompt uses a coarse GeoJS country and region lookup. Mini Golf Canada does not retain the returned IP address, coordinates or other response fields and stores only a temporary required-or-standard result for the current browser session.

Advertising storage

If advertising is activated, advertising vendors may request storage for ad delivery, frequency control, fraud prevention, reporting and personalization. In regions where consent is required, advertising storage and personalization remain denied until the appropriate consent choice is provided through a certified consent platform.

Manage choices

Select Privacy choices in the footer to change the site's analytics preference. Browser controls can also delete local storage and cookies or block them by site. Clearing browser data resets an explicit preference to the regional default; the automatic prompt is limited to regions that require it.

Questions can be sent to nocturnaldevs@gmail.com.
