Cookie Policy

Cookie categories, browser storage and privacy controls used by Mini Golf Canada.

Effective date: August 7, 2026

This policy supplements the privacy policy. Cookies are small browser files, and similar technologies include local storage, tags and device identifiers.

Necessary storage

The site may use browser storage needed for security, reliable operation and ordinary browser features. It does not store a first-party analytics preference or perform a separate browser-based region lookup.

Analytics

Google Analytics helps measure page use and directory interactions. Its tag runs on every page in advanced, region-aware Consent Mode. Analytics storage is enabled by default outside the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, Switzerland and Quebec. In those regions, analytics storage remains denied and Google receives cookieless measurement signals instead. There is no site-level analytics preference or consent prompt.

Advertising storage

If advertising is activated, advertising vendors may request storage for ad delivery, frequency control, fraud prevention, reporting and personalization. In regions where consent is required, advertising storage and personalization remain denied until the appropriate consent choice is provided through a certified consent platform.

Manage choices

Browser controls and content-blocking tools can delete or block cookies by site. The Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on can prevent Google Analytics measurement in supported browsers. Mini Golf Canada does not provide a separate analytics toggle.
