Advertising Disclosure

How Mini Golf Canada labels advertising, sponsorships and commercial relationships.

Mini Golf Canada may earn revenue from display advertising, sponsorships or clearly disclosed commercial relationships. Revenue supports research, hosting, corrections and continued directory maintenance.

Editorial independence

Payment does not purchase a positive listing description, a higher organic ranking or the removal of accurate correction information. Sponsored placements and paid links will be labelled so a visitor can distinguish them from directory content.

Display advertising

Display ads will be visually distinguishable from navigation and venue details. Advertising partners may use cookies or identifiers as described in the privacy policy and cookie policy. A visitor's consent choices apply where required.

Affiliate links

If a page uses an affiliate link that may generate a commission, the relationship will be disclosed close to the link or section. Ordinary official website, phone and map links are not presented as affiliate recommendations.

Commercial questions can be sent to nocturnaldevs@gmail.com.

Analytics preferences

Mini Golf Canada uses region-aware analytics to understand which pages help visitors. You can allow or decline analytics cookies; directory features work either way. Privacy details
