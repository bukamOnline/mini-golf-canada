Quebec City: repeat glow rounds, mini hockey or a rooftop?

Fluo S’amuse offers indoor glow play, Le Grand mixes putting and mini hockey, and Au 19e offers an outdoor rooftop with same-day replay admission. Au 19e’s updated September 29 information includes fall hours and a dated discount, but says the kitchen is closed for the rest of the season. Fluo and Le Grand figures below retain their September 7 source check.

Bring indoor shoes for snowy-weather glow golf

Fluo asks visitors to remove outdoor footwear in winter or snowy conditions to protect its carpets. Bring indoor shoes to change into, or use the operator's stated socks option. This is worth preparing for before leaving home, particularly when choosing an indoor activity because of the weather. Sources: Fluo S'amuse prices and visitor FAQ.

Allow a full activity window for Le Grand

A putting-and-hockey visit is a different commitment from fitting a few holes between errands. Use Le Grand's 90-120-minute estimate when arranging the next stop, and consult its linked parking map before the booked start. The estimate is not a guaranteed finish time. Sources: Le Grand course and booking FAQ.

Compare age mixes before selecting a family ticket

At Au 19e during the fall promotion, two adults and a four-year-old pay $37.42 individually; the $44.96 family package adds $7.54. With two adults and children aged eight and twelve, individual tickets total $59.84 and the family ticket saves $14.88 before tax. Free toddler admission changes the decision. Au 19e’s replay option is for the same course, while Fluo’s two-route access remains conditional on attendance and route accessibility. Sources: Au 19e updated fall hours, replay terms and dated discounts.
