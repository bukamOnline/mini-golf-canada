Bring indoor shoes for snowy-weather glow golf

Fluo asks visitors to remove outdoor footwear in winter or snowy conditions to protect its carpets. Bring indoor shoes to change into, or use the operator's stated socks option. This is worth preparing for before leaving home, particularly when choosing an indoor activity because of the weather. Sources: Fluo S'amuse prices and visitor FAQ.
