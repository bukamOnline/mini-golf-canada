Val-David: distinguish Les Floralies from La Falaise

The most useful correction for this local search is identity. Val-David's municipal golf directory lists Mini-golf Les Floralies at 929 Route 117, while Golf La Falaise appears as a separate business at another address. Use the named miniature course when asking about tickets and the season.
