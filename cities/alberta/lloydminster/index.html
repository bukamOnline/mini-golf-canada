Lloydminster: summer putting inside an all-seasons park

The Putting Zone appears in Discover Lloydminster's summer activities for Bud Miller All Seasons Park. The park's name can otherwise give the wrong impression: its year-round facilities and indoor aquatic centre do not establish indoor or winter miniature golf.

Keep the nature loop separate from round time

The park guide describes a 2.6-kilometre All Seasons Nature Loop. That distance and its walking estimate belong to the trail, not the putting course. For a group with limited walking tolerance, treat the loop as an optional extra after golf rather than a required part of reaching the holes. Sources: Discover Lloydminster's Bud Miller park guide.

What the destination guide cannot confirm

The source identifies the summer attraction but gives no golf tariff, equipment terms or current kiosk timetable. Ask about those three points together. A rainy-day swimming plan can still work when outdoor putting does not, but the indoor facility should not be described as a rainproof golf substitute. Sources: Discover Lloydminster's Bud Miller park guide.
