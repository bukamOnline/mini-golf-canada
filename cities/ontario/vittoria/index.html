Nearby alternative: Brantford

2026 golf-only rates: Ages 3-17 pay $6.61, adults 18-54 pay $8.33 and seniors 55+ pay $7.29. Additional rounds are paid separately. Source

Water-and-golf option: Daily Splash and Putt includes one golf round at $10.92 for youth, $14.57 for adults and $12.64 for seniors. Source
