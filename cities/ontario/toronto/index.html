Toronto: an island side trip or a downtown putting bar?

These two Toronto listings are different outings, even though both offer nine or eighteen holes. Centreville needs an island travel plan and separate golf tickets; Par-Tee Putt combines putting with a bar and separately priced table activities. Nearby Scarborough and Etobicoke courses appear under their own municipalities in this directory, so these two entries are not the full Greater Toronto selection.

Keep ferry time outside the golf estimate

Centreville directs visitors to the City of Toronto ferry and states that ferry fares are separate. Choose the round length after checking both outbound and return travel. A prepaid park pass does not reserve a ferry departure or make an eighteen-hole game fit before the next sailing.
