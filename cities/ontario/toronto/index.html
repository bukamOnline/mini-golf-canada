Questions to answer before choosing

  1. Which Toronto course is open at the exact time you plan to arrive?
  2. What is the current mini-golf-only admission for each player age, including taxes and booking fees?
  3. Are time slots, deposits or minimum group sizes required for the planned visit?
  4. Does the full course route meet the group's mobility, seating, lighting and washroom needs?
  5. Are food, arcade credits or other activities separate from the mini golf ticket?

How this Toronto page is maintained

Mini Golf Canada groups course records by their published municipality and keeps venue-controlled, municipal and tourism sources separate from public-profile context. Duplicate attraction and parent-business records are consolidated so one physical venue does not appear to be two choices.

Hours, ratings, admission and business status can change after a source check. Each course profile shows its evidence date and source links, and every page includes a correction route for operators and visitors.
