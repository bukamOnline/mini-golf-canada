Questions to answer before choosing

  1. Which Greater Sudbury course is open at the exact time you plan to arrive?
  2. What is the current mini-golf-only admission for each player age, including taxes and booking fees?
  3. Do dark rooms, music, flashing effects or animated props affect anyone in the group?
  4. Are time slots, deposits or minimum group sizes required for the planned visit?
  5. Does the full course route meet the group's mobility, seating, lighting and washroom needs?
  6. Are food, arcade credits or other activities separate from the mini golf ticket?
