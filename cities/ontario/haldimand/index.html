Dunnville day trip or a Lowbanks campground stay?

The important distinction in Haldimand is permission to play, not the number of map pins. The county lists seasonal mini putt in Dunnville, while Highland describes its course as a private resort amenity. These are not interchangeable walk-in destinations.

Avoid the wrong Dunnville destination

The county page also lists Dunnville Golf & Country Club at 150 Haldimand County Road 17. That is a regular golf course, not the mini-putt address on Broad Street East. Enter the full street address when setting directions instead of selecting the first result for Dunnville golf. Sources: Haldimand County golf directory.

What to settle before leaving

For a day trip, ask whether non-guests can play and when the last mini-putt admission is. For a resort visit, ask whether equipment and guest admission are included. Until those answers are available, neither a full golf-course green fee nor an RV campsite price is a defensible mini-golf budget. Sources: Haldimand County golf directory; Highland RV Resort amenities.
