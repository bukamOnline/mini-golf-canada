Port Dover: fit Arbortown into a waterfront walk

Arbortown's location is the practical reason to consider it for a Port Dover visit. Local directories place eighteen holes across from the Arbor and about two blocks from the beach. Those sources support a walking combination, but not an all-in price or a guaranteed opening today.

Source-based comparison by the Mini Golf Canada Research Desk, checked September 7, 2026. These are planning judgments from the linked publications, not firsthand course reviews. Prices and admission conditions can change.

September is a check, not a promise

Norfolk describes an end-of-May-through-September season, weather permitting, without a current daily schedule. That does not establish a specific final weekend. For an autumn visit, confirm the exact date rather than assuming the beach's public access means the golf course is open too. Sources: Norfolk business-directory season information.

What is not in the walking plan

The directory descriptions do not establish free parking, a reserved parking space, course accessibility or a golf-and-food bundle. Keep those costs and access questions separate. The beach proximity helps choose the shape of the outing, but it cannot resolve admission or route suitability.
