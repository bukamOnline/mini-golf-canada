The fall exception overrides the summer footer

Coast to Coast’s main course page says that from Labour Day to Thanksgiving it opens Saturdays only, 10 a.m. to dusk, weather permitting. The site-wide footer still displays summer weekday hours. Use the specific fall notice when scheduling and do not plan a September Wednesday visit around the summer discount. The golf desk number is 519-539-4921, distinct from the garden centre number.
