A useful rule for a mixed-ability Pineland group

The photographed rules cap an unfinished hole after six strokes at a recorded score of seven, then move players onward. Agreeing on that rule helps keep one difficult hole from dominating a family round. Recheck the board on arrival; the evidence is a dated photograph, not a current operator reply.
