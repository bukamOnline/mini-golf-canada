A weather interruption is not automatically a refund

Magic Mountain's stated rain-check policy concerns a whole-park suspension lasting over 60 minutes; an individual attraction closure is different. Speak to staff before leaving during an interruption. Do not buy a larger park package on the assumption that any missed golf round will be refunded.
